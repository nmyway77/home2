If the plaque ruptures, a blood clot forms on top of the tear, clogging the already narrowed pipe.This condition is called atherosclerosis, and it can have dangerous consequences. · ④: 뇌졸중까지 끝난 뒤 다시 파열로 돌아가면 흐름 역행.

[Situation] A 56-year-old patient visits your clinic with a blood pressure reading of 142 over 95 mmHg.
