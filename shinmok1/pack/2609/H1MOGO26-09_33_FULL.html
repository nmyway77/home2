The ancient Greeks felt that the visual artist's goal was to copy visual experience. This approach appears in the realism of ancient Greek sculpture and pottery. We must sadly note that, due to the action of time and weather, no paintings from ancient Greek artists exist today. We can only surmise their quality based on tales such as that of Zeuxis and Parhassios, the obvious skill in ancient Greek sculpture, and in drawings that survive on ancient Greek pottery. This definition of art as copying reality has a problem, though. Jackson Pollock, a leader in the New York School of the 1950's, intentionally did not copy existing objects in his art. While painting these works, Pollock and his fellow artists would consciously avoid making marks or passages that resembled recognizable objects. They succeeded at making artwork that did not copy anything, thus demonstrating that the ancient Greek view of art as mimesis―simple copying―does not sufficiently define art.

2. 📖 지문/근거 해석imitation 명사(mere ___ of — 본문 mimesis·simple copying) / imitate(동사 — 형용사 mere 뒤 불가) / avoid → avoided(과거, 뒤의 produced와 병렬 — 본문 consciously avoid) / avoidance(명사) / resemble(‘닮는 것을 피했다’의 목적어로는 본문 어구이나 여기서는 동사 자리에 ‘닮았다’가 되어 방향 반대) / define·survive·demonstrate 무관
3. 📖 조건/선지 해석
4. ✦ 정답 도출 + 오답 함정(A) ‘art is mere ___ of reality’ → 명사 imitation. (B) ‘deliberately ___ anything recognizable and yet produced’ — and yet 뒤 produced(과거)와 병렬 → avoided; 원형으로 두면 시제 병렬 오류. resembled를 쓰면 ‘알아볼 수 있는 것을 닮았다’로 본문과 정반대.

조건 · 보기
[주어진 문장]
Even so, we are not entirely in the dark about what they achieved.
The ancient Greeks felt that the visual artist's goal was to copy visual experience. ( ① ) This approach appears in the realism of ancient Greek sculpture and pottery. ( ② ) We must sadly note that, due to the action of time and weather, no paintings from ancient Greek artists exist today. ( ③ ) We can only surmise their quality based on tales such as that of Zeuxis and Parhassios, the obvious skill in ancient Greek sculpture, and in drawings that survive on ancient Greek pottery. ( ④ ) This definition of art as copying reality has a problem, though. ( ⑤ ) Jackson Pollock, a leader in the New York School of the 1950's, intentionally did not copy existing objects in his art.
33-10 정답 & 4단계 해설
정답 ③
1. 📖 문제 해석주어진 문장이 들어갈 위치를 고르라.
2. 📖 지문/근거 해석"그렇다 해도, 우리가 그들이 이룬 것에 대해 완전히 깜깜한 것은 아니다." — Even so(직전에 부정적 사실 필요) + they(그리스 화가들) + not entirely in the dark(직후에 '간접 근거로 추측 가능'이 이어져야 함).
3. 📖 조건/선지 해석①·②: 그리스의 복제 예술관과 그 증거 / ③ 앞: 회화가 하나도 남지 않음(부정적 사실) / ③ 뒤: We can only surmise ... based on tales(그래도 추측할 근거는 있음) / ④·⑤: 정의의 문제 → Pollock
4. ✦ 정답 도출 + 오답 함정정답 ③: 'Even so'는 직전 문장 "no paintings ... exist today"(암울한 사실)를 받고, 'not entirely in the dark'는 직후 "We can only surmise their quality based on tales ..."(이야기·조각·도기로 추측 가능)로 구체화된다. they = ancient Greek artists도 직전 문장에서 처음 등장. 함정 ②: 'realism of sculpture and pottery' 뒤에 넣으면 아직 '모른다'는 부정적 사실이 나오지 않아 Even so가 받을 대상이 없다. ④는 'they'가 가리킬 대상이 멀어지고 뒤 문장(정의의 문제)과 연결되지 않는다.

글의 흐름으로 보아, 주어진 문장이 들어가기에 가장 적절한 곳은?
조건 · 보기
[주어진 문장]
Such discipline, sustained across an entire body of work, paid off.
We can only surmise their quality based on tales such as that of Zeuxis and Parhassios, the obvious skill in ancient Greek sculpture, and in drawings that survive on ancient Greek pottery. ( ① ) This definition of art as copying reality has a problem, though. ( ② ) Jackson Pollock, a leader in the New York School of the 1950's, intentionally did not copy existing objects in his art. ( ③ ) While painting these works, Pollock and his fellow artists would consciously avoid making marks or passages that resembled recognizable objects. ( ④ ) They succeeded at making artwork that did not copy anything, thus demonstrating that the ancient Greek view of art as mimesis―simple copying―does not sufficiently define art. ( ⑤ )
33-11 정답 & 4단계 해설
정답 ④
1. 📖 문제 해석주어진 문장이 들어갈 위치를 고르라.
2. 📖 지문/근거 해석"그러한 자기 절제는, 작품 전체에 걸쳐 유지되어, 결실을 맺었다." — Such discipline(직전에 '의식적으로 피하는' 절제 행위가 서술되어야 함) + paid off(직후에 '성공했다'가 와야 함).
3. 📖 조건/선지 해석①: 정의의 문제 제기 / ②: Pollock 소개 / ③ 앞: 의도적 비복제(선언) / ④ 앞: consciously avoid making marks(작업 중의 절제 = discipline) / ④ 뒤: They succeeded(결실)
4. ✦ 정답 도출 + 오답 함정정답 ④: 'Such discipline'은 직전 문장의 'would consciously avoid making marks ... that resembled recognizable objects'(작업 내내 유지된 의식적 절제)를 받고, 'paid off'는 직후 "They succeeded at making artwork that did not copy anything"으로 구체화된다. 함정 ③: 'intentionally did not copy'도 절제로 보이지만 그 문장은 결과의 선언이고, 'sustained across an entire body of work'(작업 전체에 걸쳐 유지된)에 대응하는 과정 서술(While painting ... would avoid)은 ④ 앞에서야 나온다. ⑤는 성공이 이미 서술된 뒤라 'paid off'가 중복.
