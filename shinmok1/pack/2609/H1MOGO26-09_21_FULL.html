Human beings are built in a marvelous way, and yet we deny ourselves the very marvel we were designed for. Rather than traveling freely in every direction, most of us pass our days going nowhere at all. Day after day we sit in the same small patch of a chair for hour upon hour. A great many children today are prisoners of so-called smart devices such as phones and tablets. They do not head outdoors to play, and they do not pick up the skills of mixing with others and reading the physical signals people send. Consequently they fail to develop the stock of movements that would help them learn, communicate, create, and grow up emotionally. They are not even acquiring the basics of running, skipping, or climbing. What they acquire instead is a life alongside anxiety, depression, and even illnesses (ⓐ) [that / what] were never supposed to be part of their lives. In short, we are drifting ever further from the way we were designed. We were built for motion, and it is through motion that we are supposed to live remarkable lives. For us, to move is to live.

(D) In the summary ‘Children who are kept indoors by screens is losing the movement skills they need’, the verb ‘is’ is correct because the nearest noun is ‘screens’.

4. ✦ 정답 도출 + 오답 함정③ [한 끗] ‘달리기도 못 배운다 → 그 결과 레퍼토리를 못 쌓는다’도 인과처럼 보이지만, not even(심지어)은 더 작은 단위로 좁히는 말이라 그 뒤에 더 큰 레퍼토리 얘기가 오면 순서가 뒤집힌다.
