The human body on the Earth’s surface1 experiences2 constant air pressure3 equal to 16-18 tons per square meter of its body surface. However, this pressure is not felt4 because external5 pressure is balanced6 by internal pressure in the lungs and air cavities7 of the body. During dives, in addition8 to atmospheric pressure, the pressure of the water also acts on the human body. When submerged9 underwater, to avoid feeling10 the pressure of the surrounding environment, it11 is necessary to maintain12 the condition of equal13 external and internal pressure. To achieve this, air or a breathing mixture is14 supplied to the diver under pressure equal to the pressure applied by the surrounding water. Water pressure increases15 proportionally16 with depth17. It increases by 1 bar for every 10 meters. It follows that18 a diver at a depth of 10 meters experiences a pressure of 2 bar, and to stabilize19 pressure, the supply20 of a breathing mixture under a pressure of 2 atmospheres is required21.

1The human body on the Earth’s surface experiences constant air pressure equal to 16-18 tons per square meter of its body surface.

그러나 이 압력은 느껴지지 않는데, 외부 압력이 몸의 폐와 공기 공간 속의 내부 압력과 균형을 이루기 때문이다.

3.3점
다음 글에 대한 <보기>의 설명 중 틀린 것만을 있는 대로 고른 것은?
Every human body standing on the Earth’s surface is pressed on by the atmosphere with a steady force of about 16 to 18 tons for each square meter of skin. We do not notice it, though, because the pressure pushing in from outside is matched by the pressure of the air inside our lungs and the other air-filled spaces of the body. Under water, the weight of the water presses on the body as well, on top of the atmosphere. So, if a diver is not to feel the surrounding pressure, the pressure inside the body must be kept the same as the pressure outside. This is done by giving the diver air, or a special breathing gas, at exactly the pressure (ⓐ) [that / what] the surrounding water exerts. The deeper one goes, the greater the water pressure, rising by one bar with every ten meters of depth. A diver ten meters down is therefore under two bars of pressure and must be supplied with breathing gas at two atmospheres so that the pressures stay balanced.
<보 기>
(A) A diver ten meters down is under two bars because one bar of water pressure is added to one bar of atmospheric pressure.
(B) ‘what’ is the right choice for (ⓐ), because the clause after it is missing an object.
(C) ‘We do not notice it, though’ means that the atmosphere’s pressure on our bodies is too small to be felt.
(D) In the summary ‘Keeping the inside and the outside pressures equal are the key’, the verb ‘are’ agrees with its subject.
①(A), (B)
②(B), (C)
③(C), (D)
