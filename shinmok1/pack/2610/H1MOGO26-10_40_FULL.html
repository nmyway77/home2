( ② ) But goldenrods in more hostile territory signal to their neighbors using chemical phrases easily understood by all the goldenrod in the area, not just their biological kin. ( ③ ) It is the first time research has confirmed that these chemical communications are beneficial not only to the plant receiving them but also to the sender. ( ④ ) When times are truly tough, you do not want to be left standing in a field alone. ( ⑤ ) There will be no one to mate with, no one to help bring in pollinators.
