3.3점
다음 글에 대한 <보기>의 설명 중 틀린 것만을 있는 대로 고른 것은?
Hardly anyone looks at the world of Star Trek as an economist would, yet seeing it that way opens a window onto a future that may be closer than we think. Classical economics as a whole rests on one idea: there is normally not enough of anything to hand every person all that they want or need. For that reason, markets and prices are the tools we rely on to send goods, services, and resources to the places (ⓐ) [where / which] they are likely to do the most good. In the Star Trek world, by contrast, shortage is rare. Thanks to its technology, that society can copy whatever food it wishes, move people thousands of miles in an instant, talk across light-years, and journey between the stars. Everyone there has received a full education, which lets them take part in all this plenty. Each person works as an explorer, a researcher, an engineer, an artist, a doctor, or a counselor. Generative AI may one day make many parts of our own society just as richly supplied.
<보 기>
(A) The writer treats markets and prices as a response to the fact that there is not enough of everything for everyone.
(B) ‘where’ is the right choice for (ⓐ), because the clause that follows is complete.
(C) The sentence ‘In the Star Trek world, by contrast, shortage is rare.’ means that people there still compete for most goods.
(D) In the summary ‘Technology, not money, is what decides who gets what in Star Trek’, the verb ‘is’ agrees with its subject.

②(C)

3.2점
[A]와 [B]를 읽고 이해한 것으로 가장 적절하지 않은 것은?
[A] All of classical economics is based on the notion that there isn’t usually enough of anything to give everyone everything they want or need. Because of that, we use markets and pricing to allocate those goods, services, and resources to where they might result in the highest benefit. In Star Trek, however, there isn’t much scarcity. Technology has allowed that society to reproduce any food they want, transport themselves thousands of miles in the blink of an eye, communicate over light-years, and travel among the stars. Everyone is an explorer, researcher, engineer, artist, doctor, or counselor.
[B] Think of a village where water has to be carried from a single well. Because the well gives only so much, families take turns, pay a small fee, and argue over who deserves the most. Then one year a pipe is laid that brings water to every house without limit. Almost overnight, the rules for sharing disappear, since nobody needs to compete for what everyone now has. The village does not become careless with water; it simply stops treating water as something to fight over.
①Both [A] and [B] start from a situation in which something is not available in sufficient amounts.
②In [A] markets and prices, and in [B] turns and fees, are ways of deciding who gets what when there is not enough.
③Both texts show that once technology removes the shortage, the old way of sharing loses its purpose.
④[B] describes a change that has already happened in the village, whereas [A] describes a world that may still be ahead of us.
⑤[A] says that markets caused scarcity, and [B] says that the villagers caused the well to run low.

Q3 Everyone there has received a full education, which lets them take part in all this plenty → education.

In Star Trek, however, there isn’t much scarcity.
Few people may view the Star Trek universe through an economic lens, but doing so provides a window into a world that might soon be upon us. ( ① ) All of classical economics is based on the notion that there isn’t usually enough of anything to give everyone everything they want or need. ( ② ) Because of that, we use markets and pricing to allocate those goods, services, and resources to where they might result in the highest benefit. ( ③ ) Technology has allowed that society to reproduce any food they want, transport themselves thousands of miles in the blink of an eye, communicate over light-years, and travel among the stars. ( ④ ) All of humanity in that world has been fully educated so that they can participate in this prosperity. Everyone is an explorer, researcher, engineer, artist, doctor, or counselor. ( ⑤ ) Generative AI has the potential to allow many dimensions of our own society to be similarly highly abundant.

정답 ③
OP0삽입 문장 = S4(In Star Trek, however, there isn’t much scarcity.) · 단서: however(현실 경제와 대비) + 뒤 Technology has allowed that society(that society = Star Trek) · 함정 ② (Because of that 앞)
1. 📖 문제 해석주어진 문장이 들어갈 위치를 연결어·지시어 단서로 정한다.
2. 📖 지문/근거 해석주어진 문장의 however는 ‘현실 경제는 부족하다’는 앞 내용과 대비되어야 하므로 S2·S3(부족 → 시장·가격) 뒤. 또 뒤 문장 Technology has allowed that society의 that society는 ‘Star Trek 사회’를 가리키므로 주어진 문장이 먼저 Star Trek을 꺼내야 한다 → ③.
3. 📖 조건/선지 해석① 아직 현실 경제 설명이 없어 however가 대비할 대상이 없음 ② Because of that의 that(부족)이 주어진 문장(부족 없음)을 받게 되어 모순 ③ 부족한 현실 ↔ however Star Trek, 뒤의 that society 연결 ○ ④ that society가 가리킬 Star Trek 언급이 앞에 없음 ⑤ 결론 바로 앞은 너무 늦음
4. ✦ 정답 도출 + 오답 함정② [한 끗] S2 바로 뒤도 ‘대비’처럼 보이지만, 그러면 Because of that이 ‘부족이 없기 때문에 시장을 쓴다’가 되어 인과가 뒤집힌다. ④ Technology … that society가 먼저 나오면 that society의 지시 대상이 없다.
