Physicists, for example, must take as a given the mass and charge12 of fundamental particles. In a model of atomic bonds, physicists don’t attempt to explain13 why electrons have14 their particular mass and charge, they just accept15 the measured16 values and move on. The key point is that17 we can build useful18 models at one level of analysis without a complete19 accounting of what20’s happening21 at all other levels.

본문 문장별 해석영어 한 문장 · 우리말 한 문장
1No matter how you choose to delineate your evolutionary system of interest — let’s say it’s cell phones — there will be some things that your model just needs to take as a given, such as the fact that new ideas emerge at particular times.
여러분이 관심을 두는 진화 시스템의 경계를 어떻게 정하기로 하든 — 예를 들어 그것이 휴대 전화라고 해 보자 — 여러분의 모델이 그저 주어진 것으로 받아들여야만 하는 것들이 있을 텐데, 새로운 아이디어가 특정한 시기에 출현한다는 사실 같은 것이 그렇다.
2Indeed, all models must make simplifying assumptions of this nature.
실제로, 모든 모델은 이러한 성격의 단순화하는 가정을 세워야만 한다.
3When Wallace and Darwin presented their verbal model of biological evolution, there was little knowledge of how variation generation and inheritance worked.
Wallace와 Darwin이 생물학적 진화에 대한 그들의 언어적 모델을 제시했을 때, 변이 생성과 유전이 어떻게 작동하는지에 관한 지식은 거의 없었다.
4They just took as a given that these things happened somehow.
그들은 이러한 일들이 어떻게든 일어났다는 것을 그저 주어진 것으로 받아들였다.
5Even at the finest level of detail, we need simplifying assumptions.
가장 미세한 세부 수준에서조차, 우리는 단순화하는 가정을 필요로 한다.
6Physicists, for example, must take as a given the mass and charge of fundamental particles.
예를 들어, 물리학자들은 기본 입자의 질량과 전하를 주어진 것으로 받아들여야만 한다.
7In a model of atomic bonds, physicists don’t attempt to explain why electrons have their particular mass and charge, they just accept the measured values and move on.
원자 결합 모델에서, 물리학자들은 왜 전자가 그 특정한 질량과 전하를 갖는지 설명하려 하지 않는다. 그들은 그저 측정된 값을 받아들이고 다음으로 넘어간다.
8The key point is that we can build useful models at one level of analysis without a complete accounting of what’s happening at all other levels.
핵심은 우리가 다른 모든 수준에서 일어나고 있는 것에 대한 완전한 설명 없이도 하나의 분석 수준에서 유용한 모델을 구축할 수 있다는 것이다.

3.3점

However you decide to draw the boundaries of the evolving system you want to study — say, mobile phones — your model will have to accept certain things without explanation, for instance that fresh ideas appear at certain moments. In fact, every model has to rely on simplifying assumptions of this kind. When Wallace and Darwin put forward their verbal account of how living things evolve, almost nothing was known about how new variations arose or how traits were passed on. They simply assumed that these processes went on in some way or other. Simplifying assumptions are needed even at the most detailed level. Physicists, for instance, have to accept the mass and charge of basic particles as facts. When they model the bonds between atoms, they make no attempt to explain why an electron has the mass and charge it has; they take the measured figures and get on with the work. The essential point is that a useful model can be built at one level of analysis without a full account of (ⓐ) [what / that] is going on at every other level.
<보 기>
(A) The Wallace–Darwin example shows that a model can work even when the mechanism behind it is unknown.
(B) ‘that’ is the right choice for (ⓐ), because the noun ‘account’ just before it serves as its antecedent.
(C) ‘They make no attempt to explain why an electron has the mass and charge it has’ means physicists regard that question as unimportant for science.
(D) In the summary ‘Models at different levels do not need to explain one another’, the verb ‘do’ agrees with its subject.
①(A), (B)
②(B)
③(B), (D)

However you decide to draw the boundaries of the evolving system you want to study — say, mobile phones — your model will have to accept certain things without explanation, for instance that fresh ideas appear at certain moments. In fact, every model has to rely on simplifying assumptions of this kind. When Wallace and Darwin put forward their verbal account of how living things evolve, almost nothing was known about how new variations arose or how traits were passed on. They simply assumed that these processes went on in some way or other. Simplifying assumptions are needed even at the most detailed level. Physicists, for instance, have to accept the mass and charge of basic particles as facts. When they model the bonds between atoms, they make no attempt to explain why an electron has the mass and charge it has; they take the measured figures and get on with the work. The essential point is that a useful model can be built at one level of analysis without a full account of what is going on at every other level.

No matter how you choose to delineate your evolutionary system of interest — let’s say it’s cell phones — there will be some things that your model just needs to take as a given, such as the fact that new ideas emerge at particular times. Indeed, all models must make simplifying assumptions of this nature.

(A) Even at the finest level of detail, we need simplifying assumptions. Physicists, for example, must take as a given the mass and charge of fundamental particles. In a model of atomic bonds, physicists don’t attempt to explain why electrons have their particular mass and charge, they just accept the measured values and move on.

(B) When Wallace and Darwin presented their verbal model of biological evolution, there was little knowledge of how variation generation and inheritance worked. They just took as a given that these things happened somehow.

(C) The key point is that we can build useful models at one level of analysis without a complete accounting of what’s happening at all other levels.

OP0원문 3분할 · 단서: 주어진 글(모델은 주어진 것을 받아들임, 모든 모델이 그렇다) → (B) Wallace·Darwin 사례 → (A) Even at the finest level + 물리학자 사례 → (C) The key point 결론

3. 📖 조건/선지 해석(B) 진화 모델의 예 → (A) 가장 미세한 수준(물리학)까지 확장 → (C) 핵심 결론.
4. ✦ 정답 도출 + 오답 함정① [한 끗] (A)-(B)-(C)는 Even(~조차)이 받을 ‘덜 미세한 수준의 예’가 아직 없는 상태에서 쓰인다 — Even at the finest level은 앞의 거시적 사례가 있어야 뜻이 산다. ④·⑤ (C)의 The key point는 사례들을 모두 본 뒤의 결론이므로 중간이나 처음에 올 수 없다.
