As children, the principle of opposites1 is foreign2. Children perceive words and their meanings3 separately4 from each other. It is only in later development that5 we understood that individual words directly6 connect to one another. For many children, for instance, it is not clear that ‘Right’ is the opposite of ‘Left’. A vivid7 example of this can be seen8 when children learn to ride9 a bicycle. If parents tell10 their child “Don’t go to the left,” they will often find that the child will continue riding11 straight ahead and not automatically12 turn to the right. The same applies13 to the logical14 connection15 between ‘Yes’ and ‘No’ as perceived16 by parents. When we were children and were told, for example, “No, don’t eat with your hands,” we were confused17 and didn’t know what our parents expected from us. Our confusion18 was about whether we should continue eating or not and if so, how? Only later19 did we recognize20 the connection and understand that we should continue eating, but not with our hands but with a fork21 or a spoon.

As children, the principle of opposites is foreign. Children perceive words and their meanings separately from each other. ①children prefer words that sound foreign to words in their own language
②children cannot tell the difference between a word and its meaning
③children refuse to accept that two words can mean the same thing
④children do not yet grasp that one word is automatically linked to its opposite
⑤children learn opposite words more slowly than any other kind of word

OP0원문 3분할 · 단서: (B) It is only … / for instance(첫 예시) → (C) A vivid example of this(= Right/Left) → (A) The same applies(두 번째 예시 Yes/No)
1. 📖 문제 해석주어진 글(반대의 원리가 낯설다 · 단어를 분리하여 인식) 뒤의 순서를 지시어·연결어로 정한다.
2. 📖 지문/근거 해석(B)는 ‘나중에야 연결을 이해한다’로 주어진 글을 받고 for instance로 첫 예시(Right/Left)를 든다. (C)의 this = Right/Left가 분명하지 않다는 것 → 자전거 사례. (A)의 The same applies는 두 번째 예시(Yes/No)로 확장.
3. 📖 조건/선지 해석(B) 원리 + 예시 1 제시 → (C) 예시 1의 생생한 장면 → (A) 같은 원리가 적용되는 예시 2.
4. ✦ 정답 도출 + 오답 함정③ [한 끗] (B)-(A)-(C)는 (A)의 The same이 받을 첫 사례가 아직 없고, (C)의 this가 가리킬 Right/Left에서 멀어진다. ⑤ (C)의 this는 주어진 글의 ‘분리하여 인식한다’가 아니라 Right/Left 사례를 가리킨다.
