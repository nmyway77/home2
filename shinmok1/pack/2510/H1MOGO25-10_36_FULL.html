1Multisensory experiences are a central part of our everyday lives, yet we often take them for granted, especially when our senses function normally or are corrected to normal with aids like glasses.

정답 ④ — ⓑ correcting → corrected / ⓓ which → in which / ⓕ influences → influence
OP1seed ⓑ 수동(correcting) · ⓓ 전치사 + 관계대명사(which → in which) · ⓕ 주어 the sounds 복수(influences) / decoy ⓐ them · ⓒ seem like · ⓔ 관계대명사 that
1. 📖 문제 해석밑줄 여섯 곳 중 어법상 틀린 것을 모두 고른다. 정답은 2~3개.
2. 📖 지문/근거 해석ⓑ 감각은 안경으로 ‘교정되는’ 대상 → are corrected(수동). ⓓ we live in the world → 전치사가 필요한 자리이므로 in which(또는 where). ⓕ 문장의 주어는 the sounds(복수) — 바로 앞 the tools we use to eat은 that절 안의 일부 → influence.

밑줄 의미
2.9점
윗글의 밑줄 친 We first eat with our eyes가 의미하는 바로 가장 적절한 것은?
Consider the experience of eating a regular meal. We first eat with our eyes, but we are also exposed to countless sensory signals that influence our eating experience such as food textures, tastes, and smells. And it does not stop there. The sounds around us as we eat, the noises we make as we chew, and even the sounds of the tools we eat with all shape the experience as well.
①The appearance of food shapes our experience of it before we even taste it.
②We judge a meal mainly by how expensive it looks.
③People look at their food carefully to make sure it is safe to eat.
④Taste matters far less than people usually assume.
⑤Sight is the only sense that is involved at the start of a meal.

3.0점
다음 글의 내용을 한 문장으로 요약하고자 한다. 빈칸 (A), (B)에 들어갈 말로 가장 적절한 것은?
