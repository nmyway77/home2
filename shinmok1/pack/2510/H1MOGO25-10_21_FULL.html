Currently, urban regeneration projects in degraded areas have been promoted as improving the wellbeing of residents and solving environmental injustice problems. However, such environmental improvements in ethnic communities and/or low‑income households can . The creation of new, high‑quality green spaces can increase attractiveness, making these neighbourhoods more desirable. 정답 ①

Currently, urban regeneration projects in degraded areas have been promoted as ①improving the wellbeing of residents and solving environmental injustice problems. However, such environmental improvements in ethnic communities and/or low‑income households can create an urban green space paradox. 3. 📖 조건/선지 해석① improving 향상시키는 ② desirable 탐나는 ③ rise 오르다 ④ retention 유지·붙잡아 둠 ⑤ low 낮은
