1Currently, urban regeneration projects in degraded areas have been promoted as improving the wellbeing of residents and solving environmental injustice problems.
현재, 낙후된 지역의 도시 재생 프로젝트는 거주자들의 복지를 향상시키고 환경적 불평등 문제를 해결하는 것으로서 추진되어 왔다.
2However, such environmental improvements in ethnic communities and/or low-income households can create an urban green space paradox.
하지만 소수민족 공동체 및/또는 저소득층 가구에서 그러한 환경 개선은 도시 녹색 공간의 역설을 만들 수 있다.
3The creation of new, high-quality green spaces can increase attractiveness, making these neighbourhoods more desirable.
새로운 양질의 녹색 공간의 조성은 매력을 증가시킬 수 있어 이 지역들을 더 탐나게 만든다.
4By contrast, the cost of housing can rise, and residents may not be able to afford the rent.
대조적으로, 주거비가 오를 수 있고 거주자들은 임대료를 감당하지 못할 수도 있다.
5This results in the exclusion or displacement of the poor neighbourhood’s residents, who were intended to benefit from the ecosystem services provided by the new green space.
이는 가난한 지역 거주자들의 배제 혹은 퇴거를 야기하는데, 그들은 새 녹색 공간이 제공하는 생태계 서비스의 혜택을 받도록 의도된 사람들이었다.

다음 글에 대한 <보기>의 설명 중 틀린 것만을 있는 대로 고른 것은?
These days, projects that renew run-down parts of a city are presented as a way to make residents healthier and happier and to correct environmental unfairness. Yet when such upgrades arrive in minority or low-income neighbourhoods, they can set off what has been called the urban green space paradox. Adding new, well-designed parks and greenery makes a district more appealing, so more people want to live there. At the same time, housing becomes more expensive, and the people already living there may find the rent beyond their reach. The outcome is that the poor residents — the very people (ⓐ) [whom / for whom] the new green space was supposed to serve — are pushed out or left out. They may then be able to afford only another neglected district much like the one they came from, where green infrastructure is scarce.
<보 기>
(A) The ‘paradox’ is that new green spaces fail to make a run-down district any more attractive than before.
(B) ‘whom’ is the right choice for (ⓐ), because it is the object of the verb ‘serve’.
(C) The sentence ‘the people already living there may find the rent beyond their reach’ means that long-time residents can now afford to stay.
(D) In the summary ‘The residents forced out by rising rents often end up in a neighbourhood as neglected as their old one’, ‘forced’ is a past participle modifying ‘residents’.
①(A), (B)
②(B), (D)
③(C), (D)
④(A), (C)
⑤(A), (C), (D)
