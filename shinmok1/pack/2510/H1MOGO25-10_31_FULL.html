31-6
밑줄 의미
2.9점
윗글의 밑줄 친 The myth had taken hold가 의미하는 바로 가장 적절한 것은?
Myths aren’t only stories. For example, a well-known myth that persists today is the supposed high iron content in spinach. This is a legend that dates back to 1890 and originates from a simple miscalculation by physiologist Gustav von Bunge. He accurately determined that 100 grams of spinach contained 35 milligrams of iron but he was analyzing dried spinach, which held ten times more iron than the same amount of fresh leafy greens. Although the error was swiftly corrected, the correction was just as swiftly forgotten. The myth had taken hold. Popeye, who gained superhuman strength from the leafy greens and defended himself with iron fists, contributed to its endurance and even today, some nearly 150 years later, parents the world over use this tale to try to persuade their children into eating the healthy vegetable.
①Bunge had finally admitted his mistake in public.
②The correction had spread faster than the original error.
③People had begun to doubt that spinach was healthy.
④Scientists had stopped studying the iron in vegetables.
⑤The false belief had already become firmly established among people.

1. 📖 문제 해석take hold의 의미를 문맥으로 파악하기.
2. 📖 지문/근거 해석take hold = 자리 잡다·뿌리내리다. 앞 문장 ‘정정은 그만큼 빨리 잊혔다’ + 뒤 ‘150년 가까이 지난 지금도 부모들이 이 이야기를 쓴다’ → 잘못된 믿음이 이미 굳어졌다.
3. 📖 조건/선지 해석① Bunge가 마침내 공개적으로 실수를 인정했다 ② 정정이 원래 오류보다 빨리 퍼졌다 ③ 사람들이 시금치가 건강에 좋은지 의심하기 시작했다 ④ 과학자들이 채소의 철분 연구를 멈췄다 ⑤ 잘못된 믿음이 이미 사람들 사이에 확고히 자리 잡았다
4. ✦ 정답 도출 + 오답 함정② [방향 반전] 정정이 아니라 오류가 이겼다. ③ the healthy vegetable — 건강하다는 인식은 그대로. ① 인정 여부는 본문에 없다.

31-서2 정답 & 4단계 해설
Dried spinach held ten times as much iron as the same amount of fresh leafy greens. (제외: more, than)
OP6OP6 조건 = 불필요 2단어 제외·변형 없음·추가 없음·배수사 + as ~ as·총 16단어
1. 📖 문제 해석배수 비교 ‘배수사 + 비교급 + than’을 ‘배수사 + as + 원급 + as’로 재배열.
2. 📖 지문/근거 해석ten times more iron than → ten times as much iron as. iron은 불가산 → much. 비교급 more와 than은 사용하지 않는다.
