3.0점
다음 글의 내용을 한 문장으로 요약하고자 한다. 빈칸 (A), (B)에 들어갈 말로 가장 적절한 것은?
Myths are more than just stories. Take the widely held belief, still alive today, that spinach is unusually rich in iron. The legend goes back to 1890, when the physiologist Gustav von Bunge made a simple slip in his calculations. His measurement itself was correct — 100 grams of spinach did contain 35 milligrams of iron — but the spinach he examined was dried, and dried spinach holds ten times as much iron as an equal weight of fresh leaves. The mistake was quickly fixed, yet the fix was forgotten just as quickly. By then the myth had already put down roots. Popeye, who drew superhuman strength from the greens and fought with fists of iron, helped the story survive, and even now, close to 150 years on, parents everywhere use it to coax their children into eating the healthy vegetable.
