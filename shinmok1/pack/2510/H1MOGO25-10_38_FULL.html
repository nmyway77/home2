Humans are not the most social1 animal. Ants, bees, and termites put2 humanity to shame on many metrics3 of sociality. A wide variety4 of relatives live5 together with perfectly6 harmonious behavior and collectively7 care for their young. But while insect colonies are impressively8 social places, it’s not our kind of social life. Bees always build9 hexagonal10 hives, ants march in lines, and termites move in zigzag formations11. These patterns recur12 predictably13 because they are tightly programmed14 genetically and propelled pheromonally15. We humans are more free, less16 tightly programmed genetically, so our social patterns can be more diverse17 and dynamic. Every group dances a slightly different dance, and these choreographies18 change across generations. We still think and act in ways that19 are in harmony with others around us, but it is through patterns that are more shaped20 by nurture21, not just nature.

복합 <보기> 판별 (A)~(D)
3.3점
다음 글에 대한 <보기>의 설명 중 옳은 것만을 있는 대로 고른 것은?
Humans are not the most social of animals. On many measures of social life, ants, bees, and termites leave humanity far behind. Huge numbers of related individuals share a home, behave in perfect harmony, and raise their young as a group. Yet however impressive an insect colony is as a social place, it is not social life as we know it. Bees build hexagonal combs every time, ants travel in lines, and termites move in zigzag patterns. Such patterns repeat in predictable ways (ⓐ) [because / because of] they are fixed by genes and driven by chemical signals. We humans, being less rigidly set by our genes, are freer, so our ways of living together can take many forms and keep changing. Each group performs its own slightly different dance, and those dances are reworked from one generation to the next. We still think and act in step with the people around us, but we do so through patterns shaped more by upbringing than by nature alone.
<보 기>
(A) Hexagonal combs, lines, and zigzag patterns are given as examples of behavior that varies from one insect group to another.
(B) ‘because’ is the right choice for (ⓐ), since a full clause (they are fixed …) follows.
(C) ‘Each group performs its own slightly different dance’ means that all human groups follow a single fixed social pattern.
(D) In the summary ‘Unlike insects, whose social behavior is fixed by genes, humans develop social patterns that vary across groups and generations’, the relative ‘whose’ is used correctly.
①(A), (C)
②(B), (D)
③(A), (B), (D)
④(C), (D)
⑤(B), (C)

1. 📖 문제 해석주어진 문장(곤충 군집은 인상적으로 사회적이지만 우리와 같은 종류의 사회적 삶은 아니다)이 들어갈 곳을 찾는다.
2. 📖 지문/근거 해석But while … impressively social은 앞의 ‘인류를 무색하게 한다 · 조화롭게 함께 산다’(②까지)를 양보로 받고, ‘우리와 다른 종류’라는 전환 뒤에 그 다름의 예(벌·개미·흰개미의 고정 패턴)가 와야 한다 → ③.
3. 📖 조건/선지 해석③ 앞: 곤충 사회성의 칭찬(두 문장). ③ 뒤: Bees always build … — ‘우리와 다른 종류’의 구체적 모습. ④ 이후는 그 패턴의 원인과 인간과의 대비.
4. ✦ 정답 도출 + 오답 함정② [한 끗] 사회성 칭찬이 한 문장만 끝난 자리 — 뒤의 ‘조화롭게 함께 산다’가 여전히 칭찬이므로 전환이 너무 이르다. ④ [한 끗] 고정 패턴의 예가 이미 나온 뒤라 ‘우리와 다른 종류’라는 전환이 늦고, These patterns가 받을 문장과 떨어진다.
