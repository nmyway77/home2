In ethnic communities and/or low‑income households, however, such improvements can create what is known as an urban green space paradox. When new, high‑quality green spaces are created, a neighbourhood grows more attractive and the cost of housing goes up. Residents who cannot afford the rent are then pushed out, even though the ecosystem services of the new green space were intended for them.
