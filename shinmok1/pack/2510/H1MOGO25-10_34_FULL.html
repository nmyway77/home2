What is the Capabilities Approach (CA), and why would lawyers1 passionate about animal justice2 care about it? It is easy to say3 what it is not. The CA does not rank4 animals by likeness5 to humans or seek special privileges6 for those considered7 most “like us,” as do8 some other popular theoretical approaches. The CA has concern9 for the finch and the pig as much as the whale and the elephant. And it argues that the human form of life is simply irrelevant10 when we think about what each type of animal needs and deserves11. What is relevant is12 their own forms13 of life. Just as humans seek to be able to enjoy the characteristic14 goods of a human life, so15 a finch seeks a finch’s life and the whale a whale’s life. We should extend ourselves and learn, not lazily16 picture animals as lesser17 humans, seeking18 a life sort of like our own. According to the CA, each sentient19 creature should have the opportunity to flourish20 in the form of life characteristic21 for that creature.

What exactly is the Capabilities Approach (CA), and why should lawyers who care deeply about justice for animals pay attention to it? Saying what it is not is the easy part. Unlike some other popular theories, the CA neither grades animals by how closely they resemble human beings nor reserves special rights for those (ⓐ) [judged / judging] most ‘human-like.’ It cares about the finch and the pig no less than about the whale and the elephant. It also holds that the human way of living is simply beside the point when we ask what each kind of animal needs and is owed. What counts is the way of life that belongs to each species. Just as people want the freedom to enjoy the goods typical of a human life, a finch wants a finch’s life and a whale a whale’s. Our task is to stretch our understanding and learn, instead of lazily imagining animals as inferior humans chasing a life roughly like ours. On the CA, every creature that can feel should be given the chance to thrive in the form of life that is natural to it.
<보 기>
(A) The finch and the pig are mentioned as examples of animals that the CA values no less than large, impressive ones.
(B) ‘judging’ is the right choice for (ⓐ), because the animals are the ones doing the judging.
(C) The sentence ‘What counts is the way of life that belongs to each species.’ means that what matters is how closely an animal’s life resembles ours.
(D) In the summary ‘Each creature deserves the chance to live the kind of life that is natural to it’, the verb ‘deserves’ agrees with its subject.
①(A), (D)
②(B), (C)
③(B), (D)
④(A), (B), (C)
⑤(C), (D)

OP0원문 3분할 · 단서: (B) The CA does not …(‘아닌 것’ 설명) → (C) And it argues … What is relevant … Just as → (A) We should … According to the CA(결론)
1. 📖 문제 해석주어진 글(CA란 무엇인가 — ‘아닌 것’부터 말하기 쉽다) 뒤의 순서를 연결어·지시어로 정한다.
2. 📖 지문/근거 해석주어진 글의 what it is not을 (B)의 The CA does not rank …가 바로 받는다. (C)의 And it argues …는 (B)의 CA 설명에 덧붙이는 긍정적 주장이며, (A)의 We should …와 According to the CA …는 글을 맺는 결론.
3. 📖 조건/선지 해석(B) CA가 하지 않는 것 + 작은 동물도 중시 → (C) 인간의 삶은 무관, 관련 있는 것은 그들의 삶(Just as … so) → (A) 우리의 자세 + 결론.
4. ✦ 정답 도출 + 오답 함정② [한 끗] (B)-(A)-(C)는 결론(According to the CA …)이 가운데에 와서 (C)의 And it argues가 결론 뒤에 붙는다. ④·⑤ (C)의 And it은 앞에 CA에 대한 설명이 있어야 하므로 첫 자리에 올 수 없다.
