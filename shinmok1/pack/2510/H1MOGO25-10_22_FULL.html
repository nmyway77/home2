복합 <보기> 판별 (A)~(D)
3.3점
다음 글에 대한 <보기>의 설명 중 틀린 것만을 있는 대로 고른 것은?
People are gradually becoming more conscious of how we affect the ocean, and this growing concern is pulling into public view a discussion that should have started decades ago. That discussion, however, runs into a huge barrier. You can hardly talk about how to respond to a changing system when you have no idea, to begin with, of how that system operates. Suppose a doctor informs a patient that something is wrong with their kidneys; the patient most likely already has a rough sense of where those organs sit and what job they do. That part of their own body’s support system was covered in school. The same cannot be said of the oceans. A news report about krill numbers falling over many years in the Southern Ocean strikes us as bad news in a general way. Yet the story involves much more than hungry whales. Krill are one working part of the machinery (ⓐ) [that / what] runs the ocean. Only with some grasp of that bigger picture can we discuss the change and decide on a sensible response.
<보 기>
(A) The kidney example is given as a case where people already hold basic background knowledge.
(B) ‘what’ is the right choice for (ⓐ), because the following clause has no subject.
(C) The sentence ‘The same cannot be said of the oceans.’ means that people know their oceans as well as they know their organs.
(D) In the summary ‘The writer argues that acting on ocean news requires us to understand how the ocean works first’, the verb ‘requires’ agrees with its subject.
①(A), (C)
②(B), (C)
③(B), (D)
④(A), (B), (D)
⑤(C), (D)

[A][B] 비교 설명
3.2점
[A]와 [B]를 읽고 이해한 것으로 가장 적절하지 않은 것은?
[A] If a doctor tells a patient that they have a problem with their kidneys, the patient probably already has at least a vague idea about where their kidneys are and what they’re up to. They learned about that part of their own personal life-support system at school. But that’s not the case for the oceans. When we see a news story about the long-term decline in the numbers of krill in the Southern Ocean, it sounds generally like a bad thing. But there’s far more to it than the risk of whales going hungry. Krill are a part of the ocean engine.
[B] The same problem appears with soil. Most people can name the organs of their own bodies, but few could explain what earthworms and fungi do beneath a farm. So when we read that earthworm numbers are falling, we shrug. Yet these creatures keep the soil open to air and water, and a field that has lost them slowly hardens. Without a basic picture of how soil works, a warning about worms is just another sad headline.
①Both [A] and [B] contrast what people know about their own bodies with what they know about a natural system.
②[A] uses krill and [B] uses earthworms as examples of creatures whose decline is reported but not understood.
③In both texts, a news report about a decline fails to lead anywhere because readers lack basic background knowledge.
④[B] describes a concrete consequence of losing the creatures, whereas [A] only hints that the krill decline matters beyond whales.
⑤[A] blames schools for teaching about kidneys instead of oceans, and [B] blames farmers for ignoring earthworms.

1. 📖 문제 해석주어진 글(대화 + 거대한 장애물) 뒤에 이어질 순서를 지시어·연결어 단서로 정한다.
2. 📖 지문/근거 해석주어진 글의 a massive obstacle이 무엇인지 설명하는 것이 (B)의 첫 문장(작동 원리를 모르면 논의 불가). (B)의 the patient를 (A)의 They가 받고, (A)의 But that’s not the case for the oceans가 (C)의 해양 사례(크릴)로 이어진다.
3. 📖 조건/선지 해석(B) 장애물 설명 + 신장 예시 → (A) They(환자들) … 해양은 그렇지 않다 → (C) 해양의 예: 크릴 뉴스.
4. ✦ 정답 도출 + 오답 함정④ [한 끗] (B)-(C)-(A)는 (C)의 크릴 사례 뒤에 ‘해양은 그렇지 않다’가 와서 순서가 뒤집힌다 — (A)의 They가 가리킬 대상(the patient)이 바로 앞에 있어야 한다. ① (A)의 They가 주어진 글에 없으므로 바로 이어질 수 없다.
