Creativity is the ability to generate1 novel2 and valuable ideas. It involves divergent3 thinking, imagination4, and a willingness to experiment and take risks. While AI can be a powerful tool for creative efforts, it also carries the risk of limiting5 originality6 and innovation. AI algorithms are trained7 on existing datasets, often identifying8 patterns and trends in past creations. While this can be useful for generating new content in similar styles or formats, it can also lead to derivative9 works that10 lack genuine originality. If artists and designers rely too heavily11 on AI for inspiration and content generation, they may find themselves trapped12 in a cycle of imitation, unable to break free from the constraints13 of the AI’s training data. Moreover, the ease with which AI can generate content can discourage the kind of struggle and experimentation that often leads14 to breakthroughs. The creative process is often messy and repetitive15, involving16 numerous failures and setbacks17. It is through these challenges that we refine our skills, develop our unique18 perspectives, and push the boundaries19 of what is possible. If AI provides20 instant solutions, it can bypass this essential process of learning through trial and error, ultimately21 inhibiting the development of true creative talent.

본문 문장별 해석영어 한 문장 · 우리말 한 문장
1Creativity is the ability to generate novel and valuable ideas.
창의성은 새롭고 가치 있는 아이디어를 만들어 내는 능력이다.
2It involves divergent thinking, imagination, and a willingness to experiment and take risks.
그것은 확산적 사고, 상상력, 그리고 기꺼이 실험하고 위험을 감수하려는 마음을 수반한다.
3While AI can be a powerful tool for creative efforts, it also carries the risk of limiting originality and innovation.
AI는 창의적인 노력을 위한 강력한 도구가 될 수 있지만, 독창성과 혁신을 제한할 위험도 지닌다.
4AI algorithms are trained on existing datasets, often identifying patterns and trends in past creations.
AI 알고리즘은 기존의 데이터 세트로 훈련되어, 흔히 과거 창작물의 패턴과 경향을 찾아낸다.
5While this can be useful for generating new content in similar styles or formats, it can also lead to derivative works that lack genuine originality.
이것은 비슷한 양식이나 형식의 새 콘텐츠를 만드는 데 유용할 수 있지만, 진정한 독창성이 없는 파생적인 작품으로 이어질 수도 있다.
6If artists and designers rely too heavily on AI for inspiration and content generation, they may find themselves trapped in a cycle of imitation, unable to break free from the constraints of the AI’s training data.
예술가와 디자이너가 영감과 콘텐츠 생성을 AI에 지나치게 의존하면, 그들은 AI 훈련 데이터의 제약에서 벗어나지 못한 채 모방의 순환에 갇혀 있음을 알게 될지도 모른다.
7Moreover, the ease with which AI can generate content can discourage the kind of struggle and experimentation that often leads to breakthroughs.
게다가 AI가 콘텐츠를 생성할 수 있는 그 쉬움이, 흔히 돌파구로 이어지는 그런 고군분투와 실험을 억제할 수 있다.
8The creative process is often messy and repetitive, involving numerous failures and setbacks.
창작 과정은 흔히 엉망이고 반복적이며, 수많은 실패와 좌절을 수반한다.
9It is through these challenges that we refine our skills, develop our unique perspectives, and push the boundaries of what is possible.
우리가 기술을 연마하고, 고유한 관점을 발전시키고, 가능한 것의 경계를 넓히는 것은 바로 이러한 어려움을 통해서이다.
10If AI provides instant solutions, it can bypass this essential process of learning through trial and error, ultimately inhibiting the development of true creative talent.
AI가 즉각적인 해결책을 제공하면, 시행착오를 통해 배우는 이 필수적인 과정을 건너뛰게 하여 결국 진정한 창의적 재능의 발달을 저해할 수 있다.

3.3점
다음 글에 대한 <보기>의 설명 중 틀린 것만을 있는 대로 고른 것은?
Creativity means being able to come up with ideas that are both new and worthwhile. It calls for thinking in many directions, imagination, and a readiness to try things out and accept risk. AI can be a powerful aid to creative work, yet it also threatens to narrow originality and innovation. Because AI systems learn from datasets that already exist, they tend to pick out patterns and trends in what has been made before. That is handy for producing fresh content in familiar styles or formats, but it can also result in copied-looking work with no real originality. Artists and designers who lean too much on AI for ideas and material may end up stuck in a loop of imitation, unable to escape the limits of the data the AI was trained on. On top of that, the very ease (ⓐ) [with which / which] AI turns out content can put people off the kind of struggle and experimenting that so often produces breakthroughs. Creating something is usually untidy and repetitive, full of failures and setbacks. It is by working through such difficulties that we sharpen our skills, form our own point of view, and stretch what is possible. When AI hands us instant answers, it lets us skip this essential learning by trial and error, and in the end that holds back the growth of real creative talent.

(A) ‘Fresh content in familiar styles or formats’ is given as an example of the genuine originality that AI makes possible.
(B) ‘with which’ is the right choice for (ⓐ), because the clause that follows (AI turns out content) is complete.
(C) ‘It lets us skip this essential learning by trial and error’ means that AI removes a step the writer regards as necessary for growth.
(D) In the summary ‘Relying on AI too heavily make creators skip the struggle that builds real talent’, the verb ‘make’ agrees with its subject.

주어진 글 다음에 이어질 글의 순서로 가장 적절한 것은?
Creativity is the ability to generate novel and valuable ideas. It involves divergent thinking, imagination, and a willingness to experiment and take risks. While AI can be a powerful tool for creative efforts, it also carries the risk of limiting originality and innovation.

(A) The creative process is often messy and repetitive, involving numerous failures and setbacks. It is through these challenges that we refine our skills, develop our unique perspectives, and push the boundaries of what is possible. If AI provides instant solutions, it can bypass this essential process of learning through trial and error, ultimately inhibiting the development of true creative talent.

(B) If artists and designers rely too heavily on AI for inspiration and content generation, they may find themselves trapped in a cycle of imitation, unable to break free from the constraints of the AI’s training data. Moreover, the ease with which AI can generate content can discourage the kind of struggle and experimentation that often leads to breakthroughs.

(C) AI algorithms are trained on existing datasets, often identifying patterns and trends in past creations. While this can be useful for generating new content in similar styles or formats, it can also lead to derivative works that lack genuine originality.

②(B) - (A) - (C)
③(B) - (C) - (A)
④(C) - (B) - (A)
⑤(C) - (A) - (B)
