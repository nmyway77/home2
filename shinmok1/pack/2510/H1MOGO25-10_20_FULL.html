1One of the most important aspects of sustaining long-term relationships is communication.
장기적인 관계를 유지하는 데 가장 중요한 측면 가운데 하나는 소통이다.
2It’s easy to connect with someone and then let the relationship get stuck due to a lack of follow-up.
누군가와 관계를 맺고 나서 후속 연락이 없어 관계가 막히게 내버려 두기 쉽다.
3To keep the connection alive, make a conscious effort to stay in touch.
그 관계를 살려 두려면 연락을 유지하려는 의식적인 노력을 하라.
4This doesn’t mean constantly reaching out with requests or updates but rather maintaining a friendly and consistent line of communication.
이것은 부탁이나 근황을 가지고 끊임없이 연락하는 것이 아니라, 오히려 우호적이고 일관된 소통의 창구를 유지하는 것을 의미한다.
5A simple message to check in or share something of value can go a long way in reinforcing your relationship.
안부를 묻거나 가치 있는 것을 공유하는 간단한 메시지가 관계를 강화하는 데 큰 도움이 될 수 있다.
6For example, if you come across an article or resource that you think might interest a connection, share it with them, even if you haven’t spoken in a while.
예를 들어, 지인이 흥미로워할 것 같은 기사나 자료를 우연히 발견하면, 한동안 이야기를 나누지 않았더라도 그것을 그들과 공유하라.
7This shows that you’re thinking of them and are invested in maintaining the relationship.
이것은 당신이 그들을 생각하고 있고 그 관계를 유지하는 데 마음을 쏟고 있음을 보여 준다.

Among the things that matter most in keeping a relationship going over the years, communication stands near the top. Meeting someone is the easy part; what often happens next is that the relationship stalls simply because nobody follows up. If you want the bond to survive, you have to decide, on purpose, to keep in contact. That does not mean bombarding the other person with favours to ask or news to report; it means keeping open a warm and regular channel between you. A short note just to say hello, or to pass along something useful, does a great deal to strengthen the tie. Say you run into an article or a resource that a contact would probably find interesting: send it on, even if the two of you have not talked for some time. Doing so signals that the person is on your mind and that you care about keeping the relationship alive.
