Being1 morally good is not simply2 a matter of getting3 things right. Someone who4 tries to help others but who ends up making5 things worse6 for them, may still be morally good. Being morally good is a matter of doing the right thing ‘because it is the right thing’ but it does not depend7 on being effective8. Perhaps someone who tries but fails9 to relieve10 pain may still be morally good, although not as good as someone who has tried11 and succeeded. Nevertheless12, being morally good is not simply a matter of trying13 to do the right thing. A person who always tries to prevent14 harm but never does15, is not generally thought16 of as morally good. Doing the right thing should lead17 to some results, at least eventually18. In short, moral goodness is a mixture19 of intention20 and effect. It is a matter of trying to do the right thing but it is also a matter of getting things right to some degree21.

본문 문장별 해석영어 한 문장 · 우리말 한 문장
1Being morally good is not simply a matter of getting things right.
도덕적으로 선하다는 것은 단순히 일을 제대로 해내는 문제가 아니다.
2Someone who tries to help others but who ends up making things worse for them, may still be morally good.
다른 사람을 도우려 하지만 결국 그들의 상황을 더 나쁘게 만드는 사람도 여전히 도덕적으로 선할 수 있다.
3Being morally good is a matter of doing the right thing ‘because it is the right thing’ but it does not depend on being effective.
도덕적으로 선하다는 것은 ‘그것이 옳은 일이기 때문에’ 옳은 일을 하는 문제이지, 효과적인지에 달려 있지 않다.
4Perhaps someone who tries but fails to relieve pain may still be morally good, although not as good as someone who has tried and succeeded.
아마도 고통을 덜어 주려 애쓰지만 실패하는 사람도, 애써서 성공한 사람만큼 선하지는 않더라도, 여전히 도덕적으로 선할 수 있을 것이다.
5Nevertheless, being morally good is not simply a matter of trying to do the right thing.
그럼에도 불구하고 도덕적으로 선하다는 것은 단순히 옳은 일을 하려고 애쓰는 문제만도 아니다.
6A person who always tries to prevent harm but never does, is not generally thought of as morally good.
항상 해악을 막으려 하지만 결코 막지 못하는 사람은 일반적으로 도덕적으로 선하다고 여겨지지 않는다.
7Doing the right thing should lead to some results, at least eventually.
옳은 일을 하는 것은 적어도 결국에는 어떤 결과로 이어져야 한다.
8In short, moral goodness is a mixture of intention and effect.
요컨대 도덕적 선함은 의도와 결과의 혼합이다.
9It is a matter of trying to do the right thing but it is also a matter of getting things right to some degree.
그것은 옳은 일을 하려고 애쓰는 문제이지만, 또한 어느 정도는 일을 제대로 해내는 문제이기도 하다.

다음 글에 대한 <보기>의 설명 중 틀린 것만을 있는 대로 고른 것은?
To be morally good is not merely to produce the correct outcome. A person who sets out to help others and yet leaves them worse off can still be a good person. Goodness consists in doing what is right for the reason that it is right, and it does not hinge on being (ⓐ) [effective / effectively].
