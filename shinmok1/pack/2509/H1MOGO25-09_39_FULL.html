39-1
내용일치(영문 선지)
2.7점
윗글의 내용과 일치하지 않는 것은?
Moral goodness is not the same as getting the right result. A person who sets out to help and ends up doing harm may still be good, because what matters is doing the right thing for the sake of its rightness, not whether it works. Someone who tries and fails to ease another's pain can be good — though less good than someone who tries and succeeds. Still, good intentions are not the whole story either. A person who is forever trying to prevent harm and never once manages it is not, as a rule, called good; right action is expected to produce something, sooner or later. Goodness, then, is a blend: the will to do right, plus some measure of actually getting it right.

윗글의 내용과 일치하지 않는 것은?
To be morally good is neither simply to succeed nor simply to try. It is not simply to succeed, because a person whose help backfires can still be good; the goodness lies in doing the right thing because it is right. Yet it is not simply to try, because someone who always tries to prevent harm and never does is not generally regarded as good; doing the right thing should bring results in the end. Good intentions count, but a person who fails to relieve pain is still less good than one who succeeds. Moral goodness combines intention and effect.

Yet intention alone is not enough either.
Being good is not the same as getting the right result. ( ① ) Someone whose attempt to help makes things worse can still be good, because goodness lies in doing right because it is right, not in being effective. ( ② ) Someone who tries and fails to ease pain may be good, though less good than someone who succeeds. ( ③ ) A person who always tries to prevent harm and never does is not usually called good; right action ought to bear fruit in the end. ( ④ ) Goodness, then, is a blend of intention and effect. ( ⑤ ) It means trying to do right and, in some measure, getting it right.

What makes a person morally good?
(A) Neither answer is enough on its own. Goodness is a blend of intention and effect: trying to do right, and to some degree getting it right.
