본문 문장별 해석영어 한 문장 · 우리말 한 문장
1For a species born in a time when resources were limited and dangers were great, our natural tendency to share and cooperate is complicated when resources are plenty and outside dangers are few.
자원이 제한적이고 위험이 컸던 시기에 태어난 종에게, 나누고 협력하려는 우리의 타고난 성향은 자원이 풍부하고 외부 위험이 적을 때 복잡해진다.
2When we have less, we tend to be more open to sharing what we have.
우리가 덜 가질 때, 우리는 가진 것을 나누는 데 더 개방적인 경향이 있다.
3Certain nomadic tribes don’t have much, yet they are happy to share because it is in their interest to do so.
특정 유목 부족은 많이 가지고 있지 않지만, 그렇게 하는 것이 자신들에게 이익이 되기 때문에 기꺼이 나눈다.
4If you happen upon them in your travels, they will open up their homes and give you their food and hospitality.
여행 중에 그들을 우연히 만나면, 그들은 자신의 집을 열고 당신에게 음식과 환대를 제공할 것이다.
5It’s not just because they are nice people; it’s because their survival depends on sharing, for they know that they may be the travelers in need of food and shelter another day.
이는 그들이 착한 사람이어서만이 아니라 그들의 생존이 나눔에 달려 있기 때문인데, 자신들이 다른 날 음식과 거처가 필요한 여행자가 될 수 있음을 알기 때문이다.
6Ironically, the more we have, the bigger our fences, the more sophisticated our security to keep people away and the less we want to share.
아이러니하게도, 더 많이 가질수록 울타리는 더 커지고, 사람들을 멀리 두기 위한 보안은 더 정교해지며, 나누고 싶은 마음은 줄어든다.
7Our desire for more, combined with our decreased physical interaction with the “common folk,” starts to create a disconnection or blindness to reality.
더 많은 것에 대한 욕망은 ‘보통 사람들’과의 줄어든 실제 접촉과 결합하여 현실에 대한 단절이나 눈멂을 만들어 내기 시작한다.

밑줄 이해 (부적절)
2.9점
윗글의 밑줄 친 our walls rise higher를 이해한 것으로 적절하지 않은 것은?
When we have less, we tend to be more open to sharing what we have. Certain nomadic tribes don’t have much, yet they are happy to share because it is in their interest to do so. The irony is that as our possessions grow, our walls rise higher, our alarm systems get cleverer at keeping others out, and our wish to share shrinks. Wanting ever more, while meeting ordinary people face to face less and less, begins to cut us off from reality and blinds us to it.
①Walls stand for the barriers people put up between themselves and others as they grow richer.
②The phrase means that wealthy people own larger houses and therefore need taller walls around them.
③It is one of a series of parallel results of having more, alongside cleverer alarm systems and less sharing.
④It contrasts with the nomadic tribes, who open their homes rather than closing them off.
⑤It leads, in the writer’s view, to losing touch with ordinary people and with reality.
