Imagine you are pedalling1 your bicycle on a level2 road. You stop pedalling3: no force is now acting to move you forward. What happens? You gradually4 slow down. How could you slow down more suddenly5, in a shorter distance6? By putting the brakes on. Because the brakes change your movement, making7 you slow down more suddenly, they must be exerting8 a force on the bicycle and you, as they grip and rub on the wheel-rims. This is the force called9 friction10, which11 tends to slow down moving12 things by acting in the direction opposite13 to movement, that is backwards14. Even without the brakes on, there are15 other friction forces acting on you and your bicycle, which also slow you down. One of these is16 friction in the wheels rubbing17 on the axles18. Another is air resistance19, which you can feel, pushing20 you backwards as you and the bicycle move forwards. When you apply these ideas to something around you, like a cart, you can see what could be generating21 friction: mainly the axles rubbing on the body as they rotate.

본문 문장별 해석영어 한 문장 · 우리말 한 문장
1Imagine you are pedalling your bicycle on a level road.
평평한 길에서 자전거 페달을 밟고 있다고 상상해 보라.
2You stop pedalling: no force is now acting to move you forward.
페달 밟기를 멈춘다: 이제 당신을 앞으로 나아가게 하는 어떤 힘도 작용하지 않는다.
3What happens?
어떻게 될까?
4You gradually slow down.
당신은 서서히 느려진다.
5How could you slow down more suddenly, in a shorter distance?
더 짧은 거리 안에서 더 갑자기 느려지려면 어떻게 해야 할까?
6By putting the brakes on.
브레이크를 걸면 된다.
7Because the brakes change your movement, making you slow down more suddenly, they must be exerting a force on the bicycle and you, as they grip and rub on the wheel-rims.
브레이크는 당신의 움직임을 바꾸어 더 갑자기 느려지게 하므로, 브레이크가 바퀴 테두리를 잡고 문지르면서 자전거와 당신에게 힘을 가하고 있는 것이 틀림없다.
8This is the force called friction, which tends to slow down moving things by acting in the direction opposite to movement, that is backwards.
이것이 마찰이라 불리는 힘이며, 마찰은 움직임의 반대 방향, 즉 뒤쪽으로 작용함으로써 움직이는 물체를 느리게 하는 경향이 있다.
9Even without the brakes on, there are other friction forces acting on you and your bicycle, which also slow you down.
브레이크를 걸지 않아도 당신과 자전거에 작용하는 다른 마찰력들이 있으며, 이것들 또한 당신을 느리게 한다.
10One of these is friction in the wheels rubbing on the axles.
그중 하나는 축에 닿아 문질러지는 바퀴의 마찰이다.
11Another is air resistance, which you can feel, pushing you backwards as you and the bicycle move forwards.
또 하나는 당신이 느낄 수 있는 공기 저항으로, 당신과 자전거가 앞으로 움직일 때 당신을 뒤로 민다.
12When you apply these ideas to something around you, like a cart, you can see what could be generating friction: mainly the axles rubbing on the body as they rotate.
이 개념을 수레 같은 주변의 것에 적용해 보면 무엇이 마찰을 일으키는지 알 수 있는데, 주로 회전하면서 몸체에 닿아 문질러지는 축들이다.

Think about a sledge pulled across a snowy field. As long as you keep pulling, it moves; the moment you let go of the rope, it slides a little further and then stops. Nothing has pushed it backwards, yet it has32 lost its motion, because the runners rub against the snow and the air presses against the front. On smooth ice the same sledge travels33 much further after you let go, since there is less to rub against. Friction, not some mysterious ‘running out of force’, is what34 brings moving things to rest.

3. 📖 조건/선지 해석(B) 답 + 브레이크의 힘 → (C) 그 힘 = 마찰, 다른 마찰력들 → (A) 그중 하나·또 하나 + 수레에 적용.
4. ✦ 정답 도출 + 오답 함정③ [한 끗] (B)-(A)-(C)는 (A)의 these가 가리킬 other friction forces가 아직 나오지 않아 성립하지 않는다. ⑤ (C)의 This가 주어진 글의 질문을 받을 수 없다 — 주어진 글에는 ‘힘’이 없다.
