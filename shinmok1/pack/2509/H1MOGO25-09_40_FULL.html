①[A] says cameras are less accurate than eyes, while [B] says drawings are more accurate than photos.

Vision is influenced by our preconceptions about reality. In viewing a scene, we establish unconscious hierarchies that reflect our functional relationship to objects and our momentary priorities. For example, when visualizing a hammer in our mind's eye, we tend to “see” it in profile or at some other “ready for use” angle.
