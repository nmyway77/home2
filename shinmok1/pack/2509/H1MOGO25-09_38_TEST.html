2.7점
윗글의 내용과 일치하지 않는 것은?
Anyone who has written a long paper for school knows the need to go back and fix parts of it. On a typewriter that meant beginning again from page one; on a computer a change can be made at any moment, anywhere in the text. Video editing divides the same way. Linear editing resembles the typewriter: every clip is recorded onto tape in the order in which it will finally appear, one after another, without a pause. Nonlinear editing resembles the word processor: clips can be reordered at any time, and new ones added or pieces removed at any stage, while the rest of the video stays untouched.
38-2
내용일치(한글 선지)
2.6점
윗글의 내용과 일치하지 않는 것은?
Editing a long text used to mean retyping it; a computer lets you change any part at any time. The two ways of editing video match those two tools. In linear editing the clips go onto tape in their final sequence, continuously, so that a change late in the process is as painful as retyping a paper. In nonlinear editing the order is never locked: the editor can move clips around, insert new ones, or trim existing ones whenever necessary, and nothing else in the video is erased in the process.
