[요약] Laid end to end, the body's blood vessels would stretch about 95,000 km. Each day they carry what amounts to more than 7,500 liters of blood, yet this is the same four or five liters going round again and again. The pressure this blood puts on the vessel walls peaks when the heart contracts and reaches its floor between beats; a healthy person’s two readings normally fall within 90–120 and 60–80 mmHg. As in any plumbing, the force on the walls climbs when the fluid changes its properties, when more fluid is added, or when the pipes get narrower: thicker blood forces the heart to work harder, a salty diet holds extra water in the body, and stress hormones tighten key vessels.

  2. ⓑ Blood pressure is at its lowest while the heart is resting between two beats.
  3. ⓒ A systolic reading of 130 mmHg would still fall inside the typical healthy range.
  4. ⓓ Eating a lot of salt raises blood pressure by increasing the amount of fluid in the blood.
  5. ⓔ Stress hormones lower blood pressure by relaxing the walls of key vessels.

① 본문 표현 활용, 인칭·어형 변형.
② 단어 수: Q1 각 3단어 이내 / Q2 5단어 이내 / Q3 8단어 이내.
✦ 4단계 · 정답 도출· Q1 — 원문 In vessels that feed the heart, that will cause a heart attack / If the clot cuts off blood flow to the brain, it causes a stroke. 답: a heart attack / a stroke.
· Q2 — a stent is placed in a vessel to help hold it open. 답: hold it open (it = the vessel).
· Q3 — 문장 구조: if your blood pressure regularly rises above about 140 over 90 ~ and stays there, it can cause. it은 조건절의 주어 상황(그렇게 계속 높은 혈압=hypertension)을 받는다. 답: regularly rises above about 140 over 90 (7단어). ⚠ 오답 함정 · 감점 포인트· Q1 관사 누락(heart attack / stroke)은 학교 채점 기준에 따라 감점 가능 → a 포함 권장.
· Q2 ‘widen the vessel’: 넓히는 것은 풍선(balloon)의 역할, 스텐트는 열린 상태 유지.
· Q3 it을 elastic fibers나 blood vessels로 답하면 ‘심각한 문제를 일으키는’ 주체가 뒤바뀜. 문맥상 주어는 고혈압 상태.
