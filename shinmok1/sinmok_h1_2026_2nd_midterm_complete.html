[1, 서답형 1] 다음 글을 읽고 아래 질문에 답하시오.

When Tae Keller was a child, her grandmother often told her Korean folktales. One of Keller’s favorite stories was The Sun and the Moon, a traditional tale about two children who escape from a hungry tiger and eventually become the sun and the moon. Keller’s grandmother changed the original story in her own way. Instead of a brother and a sister, she made the characters two sisters, representing Keller and her younger sister. As they listened to the story, the girls imagined themselves fighting the tiger and escaping from it.

Years later, Keller began to wonder about the tiger in the story. She asked herself, “What if the tiger wanted something besides food? What did the tiger really want?” These questions stayed with her and eventually inspired her to write When You Trap a Tiger.

The novel tells the story of Lily, a Korean American girl who moves in with her sick grandmother. One day, a magical tiger from her grandmother’s stories appears and offers Lily a deal. Through her encounter with the tiger, Lily learns more about her grandmother, her family’s past, and her own identity.

Keller’s childhood memories became the foundation of a story that connected Korean folklore with the experiences of a modern Korean American girl. The book later won the 2021 Newbery Medal, one of the most prestigious awards in American children’s literature.

1문제해결 접근법
  1. 빈칸마다 말하는 이의 태도를 먼저 정한다. 강한 부정인지, 약한 추측인지, 후회인지 구분한다.

  3. 판단하는 대상의 때를 확인한다. 이미 지나간 일(어젯밤에 본 것, 어렸을 때의 일)이면 조동사 + have p.p., 지금의 상태에 대한 추측이면 조동사 + 동사원형을 쓴다.

(1) cannot have been (= can’t have been)
(2) may have been
(3) should have paid
(1) 진짜였을 리가 없다, (2) 진짜였을지도 모른다, (3) 주의를 기울였어야 했다. 세 칸 모두 조동사 + have p.p.이다.
추가로 가능한 답: (1) cannot be (= can’t be), (2) may be
3정답 단서
단서 1 (1) 강한 부정 · 어젯밤에 본 것
“Last night, I came face to face with a giant tiger … what I saw”

단서 2 (2) 약한 추측 · 판단을 뒤집음
“Now I wonder if what I saw … real after all”
I wonder if(~인지 궁금하다)와 after all(결국)이 있으므로 약한 추측의 may가 맞다. (1)의 판단을 뒤집는 자리이고, 주어가 what I saw(내가 본 것)로 과거의 일이므로 may have been(진짜였을지도 모른다)이 된다.
단서 3 (3) 후회 · 지나간 일
“Back then, I didn’t pay much attention to them”
그때 주의를 기울이지 않았다는 사실 뒤에 Now I realize가 이어진다. 하지 않은 일에 대한 후회이므로 should have p.p.만 가능하다.

(3) should have pay, should have payed는 틀린다. pay의 과거분사는 paid이다. should pay는 지금 해야 한다는 뜻이 되어, 그때 하지 않은 일에 대한 후회를 나타내지 못한다. (3)은 추가로 가능한 답이 없다.

Other than humans, a species of bats known as the "greater sac-winged bats" are the only mammals to engage in infant "talk," or babble. About two inches long, the greater sac-winged bats are nocturnal, sleeping through much of the day and remaining awake at night. Though they live together in groups, they keep enough distance between themselves, which enables researchers to record separately the sounds each bat makes. This is crucial to researchers who study the babbling of these greater sac-winged bats.

Recently, a group of researchers tried to analyze in detail the babbling behavior of these infant bats, also called bat pups, and to compare ⓐ them with human infant babbling. Analyzing the recordings of 216 "babbling bursts," which averaged about seven minutes but sometimes lasted as long as 43 minutes, of 20 bat pups from two bat communities in Costa Rica and Panama, the researchers found that the sounds the bat pups made were similar to ⓑ those of human infants in their repetition of syllables, the rhythmic nature of the babbling, and the universality of babbling behavior. Other similarities with the babbling of human infants included the early start of babbling, the long sequences of sounds, and the fact that the pups didn't need any stimulus from other bats — like human babies, they just babbled on, gradually acquiring more sounds.

Why do bat pups babble then? The researchers believe that babbling helps the bat pups prepare for and learn about the elements in courtship songs sung by adult bats. When the female pups learn through babbling the basic building blocks of a courtship song, for example, such knowledge later makes ⓒ them better judges of male song when they have grown beyond their infancy. Adult males, on the other hand, compete intensely to attract females using their bat-song, the components of which they learned about in their infancy while babbling. Ultimately, the females choose which males they prefer, and the males constantly court ⓓ them in a kind of talent competition.

It appears that such babbling practice may not only prepare bat pups for courtships, but also help them develop the vocal skills necessary for communication as adults. Since the pups learn and practice different sounds during infancy without needing direct encouragement from other bats, their babbling may play an important role in the development of their vocal communication. Because these patterns are similar to ⓔ those observed in human infants, studying bat pups may help researchers better understand how young animals learn to communicate and how vocal behaviors develop early in life.

Such common characteristics may yield valuable insights for better understanding the essential nature and development of vocal communication not only of humans but, ultimately, of non-human species as well.

26. 다음 대화를 아래와 같은 표로 정리할 때, 대화의 내용과 일치하지 않는 것은? [2.7점]

A: Did you see that article about how slang has changed because of the internet? It said the whole dynamic used to be totally different before social media existed.

B: Right, back then the "rule makers" of language were in charge — the people who decided what counted as proper, Standard English. If you wanted the public to hear you, you had to follow their strict rules.

A: Exactly. So basically only print and broadcast media had a voice, and outlets like TV news enforced those norms strictly. That’s why you’d never hear a TV presenter or journalist using slang on air.

B: But then social media completely broke that barrier, right? Now literally anyone can become a public figure online, and there’s no one left to enforce the old rules.

A: That’s the key shift. Content creators — unlike traditional broadcasters — can freely use slang like "function" or "side-eye." And because these creators have real influence, using those words actually legitimizes them and helps them spread to a wider audience faster than before.

B: There’s also a diversity angle. Content creators now come from a much wider range of backgrounds than people who used to work in traditional media, so slang isn’t limited by the economic and social barriers that held it back in the past.

A: Right — and on top of that, the loose social networks that used to connect people mainly by geography are now much stronger online, which is why memes and new slang terms travel so fast these days.

②Print and broadcast media enforced strict language normsSocial media removed enforcement of those norms
③TV presenters and journalists avoided using slangContent creators regularly use slang and help legitimize it
④Slang spread quickly across economic and social boundariesSlang was mostly confined to traditional media professionals
⑤Social networks were limited mainly by geographyOnline networks are stronger and spread memes rapidly

ANSWER · 정답
1문제해결 접근법
  1. 표의 두 칸이 “이전”과 “이후”라는 점을 먼저 확인한다.
  2. 대화에서 과거를 나타내는 말(back then, used to, in the past)과 현재를 나타내는 말(now, these days)에 표시한다.
  3. 표의 각 줄을 대화의 해당 발언과 대조하고, 이전과 이후가 뒤바뀌지 않았는지 본다.
  4. 내용 자체가 대화에 없는 것인지도 함께 확인한다.

④
과거에는 경제적·사회적 장벽이 속어의 확산을 막았다. “이전에 빠르게 퍼졌다”는 것은 반대이고, “이후” 칸도 대화와 맞지 않는다.
3정답 단서
단서 1 과거의 속어
“the economic and social barriers that held it back in the past”
과거에는 장벽이 속어를 억눌렀다. 장벽을 넘어 빠르게 퍼진 것이 아니다.
단서 2 현재의 속어
“Content creators now come from a much wider range of backgrounds”
지금은 훨씬 다양한 배경의 창작자들이 속어를 쓴다. 전통 매체 종사자에게 한정된다는 것은 대화와 다르다. 오히려 전통 매체에서는 속어를 쓰지 않았다.
4오답 분석
① 일치한다. 과거에는 언어의 규칙을 정하는 사람들이 주도했고, 지금은 누구나 온라인에서 공인이 될 수 있다.
② 일치한다. 인쇄·방송 매체가 규범을 엄격히 지켰고, 소셜 미디어가 그 장벽을 깨서 옛 규칙을 강제할 사람이 없다.
③ 일치한다. TV 진행자나 기자는 방송에서 속어를 쓰지 않았고, 창작자들은 속어를 자유롭게 써서 정당성을 부여한다.
⑤ 일치한다. 주로 지리로 사람을 잇던 느슨한 관계망이 온라인에서 훨씬 강해져 밈과 속어가 빠르게 퍼진다.

2.7점
27. 밑줄 친 빈칸에 들어갈 문장으로 가장 적절한 것은? [2.7점]

We are wonderfully made, yet we don’t allow ourselves to participate in the wonder for which we were created. Instead of moving anywhere and everywhere, most of us spend our time moving nowhere. We occupy the same square footage of a chair for hours and hours every day. Many kids today are being held captive by ‘smart’ devices like phones and tablets. They are not going outside to play, they are not learning how to socialize and read physical cues from others. As a result they are not learning or building their movement repertoire, the very thing that will help them learn, communicate, create, and become emotionally mature. They are not even learning how to run, how to skip, or how to climb. Instead, they are learning how to live with anxiety, depression, and even diseases they were never meant to have. The point is                 . We were made to move — and through movement, we are meant to live extraordinary lives. Movement is life to us.

① technological advances have successfully liberated humans from physical labor ② modern individuals are increasingly deviating from their natural physical design ③ physical health is far more crucial than emotional maturity in child development ④ digital devices have provided new avenues for emotional communication ⑤ sedentary habits are an unavoidable byproduct of human intellectual evolution

ANSWER · 정답
1문제해결 접근법

  2. 빈칸 앞의 흐름을 요약한다. 우리는 놀랍게 만들어졌지만 움직이지 않고 있다.
  3. 빈칸 바로 뒤 문장과도 이어져야 한다. “우리는 움직이도록 만들어졌다.”

②

③ 신체 건강과 정서적 성숙을 비교하지 않았다. 오히려 움직임이 정서적 성숙을 돕는다고 했다.
④ 디지털 기기에 대한 긍정적 평가이다. 지문은 아이들이 기기에 사로잡혀 사회성을 배우지 못한다고 했다.
