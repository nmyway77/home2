문단 ⓐ · 마커 1~12 (12개)
Tigers used to be ________(abundance)1) in Korea, and they often [appeared / were appeared]2) in folktales. Sometimes they were shown in a ____________3) light and other times as ________(fool)4) creatures. People respected the ____________5) and strength of tigers, but they were also [afraid of / afraid to]6) them. So they made tigers foolish characters in their stories [to be / being]7) less scared of them. The population of tigers ________(decline)8) during the Joseon Period, and they almost became ________(extinction)9) during the Japanese colonial era. But their popularity in Korean culture has recently been ________(revive)10). This is partly because they were chosen as ________(mascot)11) for the Olympics. They were also the ____________12) of a recent K-pop song.
정답 & 마커별 상세 해설 펼치기
1)어형abundantused to be ___ : be동사 보어 자리 → 형용사. 명사형 abundance 제공 → abundant. 시험지 서1 (1)과 동일 변형.
2)어법appearedappear는 자동사 → 수동태(were appeared) 불가.
3)빈칸positivein a positive light(긍정적 시각) — foolish와 대비되는 핵심어. 시험지 서1 (2).
4)어형foolishcreatures를 수식하는 형용사 → 원형 fool(명사)에서 foolish. 서1 (3).
5)빈칸couragethe courage and strength — 존경의 대상 핵심어. 서1 (4).
6)어휘afraid ofbe afraid of + 명사(them). afraid to는 뒤에 동사원형.
7)어법to be목적(~하기 위해)의 to부정사. 문맥상 '덜 무서워하기 위해'.
8)어형declined조선 시대의 과거 서술 → 과거형. 서1 (5).
9)어형extinctbecame의 보어 → 형용사 extinct. 명사형 extinction 제공. 서1 (6).
10)어형revivedhas recently been ___ : 현재완료 수동 → p.p. 서1 (7).
11)어형mascotschosen as ___ for the Olympics(두 올림픽) → 복수. 서1 (8).
12)빈칸subjectthe subject of a song = 노래의 소재. 요약문 어휘 그대로.

문단 ⓑ · 마커 13~19 (7개)
[영작·전부 제공] 오래전, 한국에 호랑이가 매우 많아서 사람들은 종종 우리나라를 '호랑이의 땅'이라고 불렀다 (often / abundant / the “Land of Tigers” / people / be / so / the country / call / long ago / that / in Korea / tigers)13) In one old tale, the tiger was [so / such]14) hungry that it crept down into the village. [순서배열] (run away / so / it / frightened / the tiger / that / be)18). it19) never returned to that village again.
정답 & 마커별 상세 해설 펼치기
13)영작Long ago, tigers were so abundant in Korea that people often called the country the “Land of Tigers.”be→were(복수·과거) · call→called. so + abundant + that. 시험지 서2 표적 문장(단어 전부 제공, 스크램블 0.75).
14)어법soso + 형용사(hungry) + that. such 뒤에는 명사구가 와야 함.
15)어법sovery는 that절과 호응 불가 → so loudly that.
16)어법that뒤에 완전한 절(the tiger could hear it) → 접속사 that. which는 불완전한 절을 이끔.
17)어법so wellwell은 부사 → so well that. such는 명사 앞.
18)순서The tiger was so frightened that it ran awaybe→was · run→ran. 두 문장을 so~that으로 결합하는 프린트 C유형 훈련(호랑이 소재).
19)지칭it (= the tiger)도망친 뒤 다시 돌아오지 않은 주체 = the tiger.

문단 ⓒ · 마커 20~26 (7개)
Why do so many Korean tales laugh at the tiger? People ________(may / turn)20) the beast into a clown simply to make it less terrifying. Hunters of the Joseon period [must have killed / must kill]21) a great many tigers, since the population fell so [sharply / sharp]22). In the gotgam story, the tiger [cannot have known / must not have known]23) what a dried persimmon was. Looking back, we ________(should / protect)24) the animal before it disappeared. Even the Bronze Age rock art [might have been carved / might have carved]25) by hunters who both feared and ________(admire)26) their prey.
정답 & 마커별 상세 해설 펼치기
20)어형may have turned과거 사실에 대한 추측 → may + have + p.p.(turned).
21)어법must have killed개체 수 급감이라는 현재 근거로 과거를 강하게 확신 → must have p.p.
22)어법sharply동사 fell을 수식 → 부사 sharply.
23)어법cannot have known'~했을 리가 없다' — must have p.p.의 반대짝은 must not이 아니라 cannot have p.p.
24)어형should have protected후회(했어야 했는데 안 했다) → should + have + p.p.
25)어법might have been carved바위그림은 '새겨지는' 대상 → 수동 have been p.p.
26)어형admiredwho both feared and ___ : feared(과거)와 병렬 → admired.
