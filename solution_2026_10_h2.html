QUESTION 2 · 대화의 의견 파악정답 & 상세풀이
1문제해결 접근법
  1. 의견을 묻는 사람(남자, 아빠)의 말에 집중한다.
  2. should, need to가 들어간 문장을 찾는다.
  3. 이유(돌발 상황, 여유 있게 즐기기)는 근거일 뿐이다. 근거로 뒷받침하는 주장이 의견이다.
  4. 근거에 나온 단어(unexpected situations)를 이용한 선지(보험)를 조심한다.
2정답
②정답
아빠는 교통 지연 같은 돌발 상황에 대처하고 각 활동을 여유 있게 즐기도록 활동 사이에 시간을 두라고 두 번 말한다.

4오답 분석

③ 많은 활동을 하고 싶은 것은 딸의 생각이다. 아빠는 오히려 여유를 두라고 한다.

21.밑줄 친 a bird in the hand is worth two in the bush가 다음 글에서 의미하는 바로 가장 적절한 것은? [3점]

To see how borrowing and lending changes a person’s worldview, consider what the rate of interest does to people’s perception of time. Imagine you are lending to somebody at an interest rate of 10 percent per year for five years. This rate tells us that the money lent has a cost that reflects the risk of you not being repaid and the opportunity cost of not spending that money yourself. The longer the term of the loan, the higher the chance you won’t be paid back because it is further into the future ― which by definition is unknowable ― and the longer you will have to wait before spending the money yourself. In order to make this worthwhile for the lender, money has to have a price ― a cost to the borrower and income to the lender. This price factors in the value of time. To put it another way, a bird in the hand is worth two in the bush.

* factor in: ~을 감안하다
① Lenders and borrowers benefit from each other.
② Spending money now is wiser than keeping it for later.
③ The lender decides the interest rate for the borrower.
④ Borrowing money involves the risk of damaging one’s credit.
⑤ Money you have now is more valuable than the same amount later.

23.다음 글의 주제로 가장 적절한 것은?

Research on cephalopod neurobiology suggests that an octopus possesses roughly five hundred million neurons. What makes this system unusual is where many of those neurons are located. More than half are distributed throughout the arms rather than concentrated entirely in the central brain. Each arm contains networks of nerve cells capable of controlling complex movements and responding to sensory input locally. This means that many actions do not require detailed instructions from the brain itself. Instead, the arms function as highly capable extensions of the nervous system that interact with the environment directly. The suckers lining each arm play an important role in this process. These structures provide strong grip but also detect chemical signals on surfaces they contact. Marine biologists studying octopus behavior have shown that this allows the animal to gather chemical information through touch. In practical terms, an octopus can effectively taste what it touches.

(A)She kept thinking about Victoria Gray, the brilliant violinist who had performed at Maestro Hall.그녀는 Maestro Hall에서 공연했던 뛰어난 바이올리니스트 Victoria Gray를 계속 생각했다. [45 ①]

(B)She focused on practicing (b) her violin, trying to find herself, for a week.그녀는 자신을 찾으려 애쓰며 일주일 동안 (b) 그녀[Ann]의 바이올린 연습에 집중했다.
(B)On Saturday, Ann picked up her violin and played a simple piece she’d known since childhood.토요일에 Ann은 바이올린을 들고 어릴 때부터 알던 간단한 곡을 연주했다. [45 ③ 처음 접한 곡 ×]

(B)When she finished, her little sister Emma clapped enthusiastically. “When (c) you play, it makes the whole house feel nicer.”연주가 끝나자 여동생 Emma가 열렬히 손뼉을 쳤다. “(c) 언니[Ann]가 연주하면 온 집이 더 좋아지는 것 같아.”
(B)Ann smiled, finally at peace.Ann은 마침내 마음이 편안해져 미소 지었다.
(C)Hearing her words, Mrs. Jones smiled softly and said, “That’s because you’re measuring yourself against someone else’s ruler. Comparison steals your joy and unique voice. Stop looking at (d) her journey and focus on your own.”그녀의 말을 듣고 Jones 선생님은 부드럽게 미소 지으며 말했다. “그건 네가 다른 사람의 자로 자신을 재고 있기 때문이야. 비교는 너의 기쁨과 고유한 목소리를 빼앗아. (d) 그녀[Victoria]의 여정을 보는 걸 멈추고 너 자신의 여정에 집중하렴.”
(C)Hearing those comforting words, something profound shifted inside Ann.그 위로의 말을 듣고 Ann의 마음속에서 무언가 깊은 것이 바뀌었다.
(C)After the lesson, Ann took down Victoria Gray’s picture that had always been on her bedroom wall.레슨이 끝난 뒤 Ann은 늘 침실 벽에 붙어 있던 Victoria Gray의 사진을 떼었다. [45 ④]
(D)What was the point? Ann thought. I’ll never be like her.무슨 소용이 있어? Ann은 생각했다. 나는 절대 그녀처럼 될 수 없을 거야.
(D)As the lesson began, her teacher, Mrs. Jones, immediately noticed Ann’s distraction.레슨이 시작되자 선생님인 Jones 선생님은 Ann이 산만하다는 것을 바로 알아차렸다. [45 ⑤]
(D)“Is everything alright, Ann?” asked Mrs. Jones.“괜찮니, Ann?” Jones 선생님이 물었다.
(D)“I’m just wondering if I’m good enough to be special like Victoria,” Ann said. “I used to love playing. Now (e) I just feel like I’m not good enough. I don’t have the perfect technique like her.”“제가 Victoria처럼 특별해질 만큼 잘하는지 궁금할 뿐이에요.” Ann이 말했다. “예전엔 연주하는 걸 정말 좋아했어요. 이제 (e) 저[Ann]는 그냥 충분히 잘하지 못하는 것 같아요. 저는 그녀처럼 완벽한 기술이 없어요.”
