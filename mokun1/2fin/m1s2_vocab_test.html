[1~2] 영영풀이

1
빈칸에 들어갈 단어의 영영풀이에 해당하지 않는 것은? (4점)
• Living without plastic for a week is a tough ________, but it's worth trying.
• When we ________, we often leave a lot of food uneaten.
• Turning off unused lights can ________ your electricity bill.
• Bringing your ________ tumbler saves hundreds of paper cups every year.
① something that needs great mental or physical effort to be done successfully
② to eat in a restaurant
③ v. to make something less or smaller
④ a. belonging to a particular person or thing
⑤ n. a living thing that has leaves and roots
정답·해설 보기
정답 ⑤
📖 해석

빈칸: challenge(도전) · eat out(외식하다) · reduce(줄이다) · own(자신의) → ⑤는 plant(식물)의 풀이.

📌 근거

[2025 1-2 중간 #1형] 프린트 예문 4개 + 풀이 5개.

2
단어와 영영풀이가 바르게 연결된 것은? (3점)
① recycle: v. to use something again
② plog: v. to pick up trash while jogging
③ reuse: v. to process used things so that they can be used again
④ pick up: to give something to someone without asking for money
⑤ tear: v. to use a needle and thread to make or fix clothes
정답·해설 보기
정답 ②
📖 해석

① reuse의 풀이 ③ recycle의 풀이 ② plog ○ ④ give away의 풀이 ⑤ sew의 풀이

📌 근거

[2025 기말 #6형] recycle ↔ reuse 풀이 교차가 핵심. recycle = 처리(process)해서 다시 쓰게 함 / reuse = 그대로 다시 씀.

[3~6] 다음 글을 읽고 물음에 답하시오.
Minji joined a zero-waste ⓐchallenge last month. She wanted to protect the ⓑenvironment, so she decided to make (A)[less / more] waste. First, she brought her ⓒown cup to school every day. Second, she washed her plastic bags and tried to (B)[reuse / produce] them. She also stopped ordering food (C)[delivery / machine] because it made a lot of trash. One day, she put empty bottles into a recycling ⓓmachine and got some points. Then she asked her friends to do her a ⓔfavor and join her. "Small actions can help the Earth!" she said.
3
윗글의 (A), (B), (C)에서 문맥상 알맞은 것끼리 짝지어진 것은? (3점)
① less — reuse — machine
② more — produce — delivery
③ less — produce — delivery
④ less — reuse — delivery
⑤ more — reuse — machine
정답·해설 보기
정답 ④
📖 해석

(A) 환경을 보호하려고 쓰레기를 덜 만들기로 했다 (B) 비닐봉지를 씻어 재사용하려 했다 (C) 음식 배달 주문을 그만두었다

📌 근거

Words & Expressions C 원문: make less waste / tried to reuse them / stopped ordering food delivery.

7
두 단어(표현)의 관계가 나머지 넷과 다른 것은? (3점)
① completely — fully
② in return — in exchange
③ single-use — disposable
④ secondhand — brand new
⑤ leftover — remaining food
정답·해설 보기
정답 ④
📖 해석

①~⑤ 같은 뜻(완전히 / 대가로 / 일회용의 / 남은 음식) ④ 중고의 ↔ 완전 새것인 (반대)

8
밑줄 친 waste가 학교 프린트의 waste(n. things that people throw away because they are not needed)와 같은 의미로 쓰인 것끼리 짝지어진 것은? (4점)
ⓐ Don't waste your time on your phone.
ⓑ Buying that toy was a waste of money.
ⓒ We should reduce food waste at school.
ⓓ Plastic waste often ends up in the sea.
ⓔ He wasted a lot of water.
① ⓐ, ⓑ
② ⓒ, ⓓ
③ ⓐ, ⓔ
④ ⓑ, ⓒ
⑤ ⓑ, ⓒ, ⓓ
정답·해설 보기
정답 ②
📖 해석

ⓐ 휴대폰에 시간을 낭비하지 마(동사) ⓑ 그 장난감을 산 건 돈 낭비였다(명사, 낭비) ⓒ 학교에서 음식물 쓰레기를 줄여야 한다 ○ ⓓ 플라스틱 쓰레기는 종종 바다로 간다 ○ ⓔ 그는 물을 많이 낭비했다(동사)

📌 근거

waste ① n. 쓰레기(프린트) ② n. 낭비 ③ v. 낭비하다. ⓑ는 명사지만 ‘낭비’라 다른 뜻.

• The kitten (A)________ my old sweater yesterday.
• I sewed a patch on my (B)________ jeans.
① tore — torn
② teared — torn
③ tore — tore
④ torn — tore
⑤ teared — teared
정답·해설 보기
정답 ①
📖 해석

(A) 새끼 고양이가 어제 내 낡은 스웨터를 찢었다 — 과거형 tore (B) 찢어진 청바지에 천을 덧대 꿰맸다 — 과거분사(형용사) torn

ⓑ Please pick it up before you leave.
ⓒ I gave them away to kids in need.
ⓓ I gave away them to kids in need.
ⓔ We picked up the litter one by one.
① ⓐ, ⓒ
② ⓑ, ⓓ
③ ⓑ, ⓒ, ⓔ
④ ⓐ, ⓓ, ⓔ
⑤ ⓒ, ⓓ, ⓔ
정답·해설 보기
정답 ③
📖 해석

ⓑ 떠나기 전에 그것을 주워 주세요 ⓒ 나는 그것들을 도움이 필요한 아이들에게 나눠 주었다 ⓔ 우리는 쓰레기를 하나씩 주웠다

13
우리말을 영어로 바르게 옮긴 것은? — "나는 쓰던 만화책을 학교 도서관에 무료로 주었다." (3점)
① I gave away my old comic books to the school library.
② I gave up my old comic books to the school library.
③ I took away my old comic books from the school library.
④ I gave in my old comic books to the school library.
⑤ I gave away them to the school library.
정답·해설 보기
정답 ①
📖 해석

① give away 나눠 주다 ○ ② give up 포기하다 ③ take away 가져가 버리다 ④ give in 굴복하다/제출하다 ⑤ 대명사 어순 ✗(gave them away)

📌 근거

W&E E-6. give away = to give something to someone without asking for money.

① sewing a cute bag from old curtains
② turning old jeans into a pencil case
③ turning an old sweater into a cleaning cloth
④ buying a new plastic bag at the store

15
Writing 연습 프린트의 5R 원칙과 실천이 바르게 연결되지 않은 것은? (3점)
① Refuse — saying no to single-use straws and flyers
② Reduce — using cloth bags to make less packaging
③ Reuse — using a tumbler many times
④ Recycle — sorting bottles and cans properly
⑤ Rot — giving away old toys to children in need
정답·해설 보기
정답 ⑤
📖 해석

Rot(썩히다)은 음식물 쓰레기·자연 소재를 흙으로 돌려보내기. ⑤ 헌 장난감 나눠 주기는 Reuse(재사용)에 가깝다.

📌 근거

Writing STEP 1 표: Refuse 받지 않기 → Reduce 덜 쓰기 → Reuse 여러 번 쓰기 → Recycle 분리수거 → Rot 퇴비.
