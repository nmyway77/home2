[2025 중간 #1형] 빈칸에 들어갈 단어의 영영풀이에 해당하지 않는 것은? (4점)
• The volunteers picked up the litter ________.
• Always check the ________ to find out if a bottle is recyclable.
• Cars ________ gases that make the Earth warmer.
• You can ________ glass jars as pencil holders or flower vases.
① one person or thing following another in order
② n. a piece of paper or cloth on an item that gives information about it
③ v. to make or grow something
④ v. to use something again
⑤ v. to process used things so that they can be used again
정답·해설·해석
정답 ⑤
📖 해석

빈칸: one by one(하나씩) · label(라벨) · produce(생산하다, 내뿜다) · reuse(재사용하다) → ⑤는 recycle(재활용하다)의 풀이로 어느 빈칸에도 해당하지 않는다.

📌 근거

프린트 Word List 15·11·20·23번 예문 그대로. reuse(그대로 다시 씀) ↔ recycle(처리해서 다시 쓰게 함) 풀이가 짝으로 함정.

[2] 다음 글을 읽고 물음에 답하시오.
Every day, we produce lots of ⓐwaste, and the Earth is getting sicker and sicker. Do you want to save the Earth? Then, join the zero-waste ⓑchallenge, and share your stories.
Olivia: My birthday was last Saturday, and my family and I ate out. There were lots of ⓒleftovers, but we brought them home. The next day, I made a nice dinner out of them.
Domingo: My dog ⓓtore my bag. I wanted to buy a new one, but I thought again. … So, I decided to fix it and reuse it. I'm not very good at ⓔsewing, and I don't think that the bag looks perfect.

[2025 중간 #23형] 다음 글의 (A)~(C)의 영영풀이로 알맞은 것끼리 짝지어진 것은? (3점)
I heard that plastic never goes away (A)completely. I didn't use any (B)single-use plastic today. … I put them into the machine one by one, and I got some points (C)in return.
① in every way — made to be used only once — as a response, exchange, or reward for something
② in every way — used by someone else before — as a response, exchange, or reward for something
③ one person or thing following another in order — made to be used only once — as a response, exchange, or reward for something
④ in every way — made to be used only once — to give something to someone without asking for money
⑤ one person or thing following another in order — used by someone else before — to give something to someone without asking for money
정답·해설·해석
정답 ①
📖 해석

나는 플라스틱이 결코 완전히 없어지지 않는다고 들었다. 나는 오늘 일회용 플라스틱을 하나도 쓰지 않았다. … 나는 그것들을 기계에 하나씩 넣고 그 대가로 포인트를 받았다.

📌 근거

(A) completely = ad. in every way (B) single-use = a. made to be used only once (C) in return = as a response, exchange, or reward for something. 오답 풀이는 같은 프린트의 one by one · secondhand · give away 풀이.

빈칸에 알맞은 말을 Word List에서 골라 쓰시오. (필요하면 어형을 바꿀 것)
(1) My grandma freezes the ____ and eats them later. → leftovers
(2) The kitten ____ my old sweater, so I turned it into a cleaning cloth. → tore
(3) I ____ a patch on my torn jeans instead of buying new ones. → sewed
(4) Many teenagers ____ in the park to stay fit and keep it clean. → plog
정답·해설·해석
정답 (1) leftovers (2) tore (3) sewed (4) plog
📖 해석

(1) 할머니는 남은 음식을 얼려 두었다가 나중에 드신다. (2) 새끼 고양이가 내 낡은 스웨터를 찢어서, 나는 그것을 걸레로 만들었다. (3) 나는 새 청바지를 사는 대신 찢어진 청바지에 천을 덧대어 꿰맸다. (4) 많은 십대들이 건강을 유지하고 공원을 깨끗하게 하려고 플로깅을 한다.

📌 근거

셀 수 있는 leftover → them으로 받으므로 복수 / tear-tore-torn / sew-sewed / 복수 주어 + 현재 plog. 프린트 W&E B-12, Word List 27·25·19번 원문.
