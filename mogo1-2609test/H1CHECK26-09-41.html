ⓐ
The role of ‘slang’ in popular culture has __________(fundamental)1) [changed / been changed]2) [because of / because]3) the internet. Since the ____________4) of Standard English, the rule makers of language [have been / has been]5) in charge, and their ____________6) norms about a ‘____________7)’ version of the English language have [dominated / been dominated]8) our channels of communication. If you wanted your voice [to be heard / to hear]9) by the public, you had to work within their10) limits, __________(conform)11) to the [strict / loose]12) expectations of [either / neither]13) print or broadcast media. Social media completely [broke / broken]14) that barrier15).
ⓑ
The playing field is now [level / uneven]16), and public access is __________(democracy)17). [영작·전부 제공]누구나 새로운 소통 플랫폼에서 공인이 될 수 있다 (the new communicative platforms / a public figure / can / anybody and everybody / on / become)18), [meaning / means]19) that nobody’s left to ____________20) the old rules. TV presenters and journalists might not have [been / be]21) able to use slang, but content creators [can / cannot]22). We regularly use words like ‘function’ and ‘side-eye,’ and our influence both __________(legitimize)23) these terms and [gives / give]24) them [순서배열](than / spread / slower / a large platform / they could have / to / before)25).
ⓒ
Content creators also come from a [greater / narrower]26) ____________27) of backgrounds than people __________(work)28) in traditional media. This29) means that slang can more [naturally / natural]30) go beyond the economic and social ____________31) [that / what]32) once [limited / boosted]33) its growth. The loose social networks that previously connected us geographically [are / is]34) simply stronger online, and [순서배열](transmit / at rapid pace / receive / be / memes)35).
정답 & 해설 펼치기 (Answer Key 1~35)
ANSWER KEY · 정답
1)fundamentally(부사 — 원형 fundamental 제공)
2)changed(자동사 ‘변하다’ 현재완료 — 수동 불가)
3)because of(전치사+명사 the internet)
4)emergence(등장 — 서7 (A) 표적)
5)have been(주어 the rule makers 복수 — 선4 ① 표적)
6)prescriptive(규범적 — 서1 (1)·서6 (A) 표적)
7)correct(‘올바른’ 판본)
8)dominated(능동 — 규범이 채널을 지배)
9)to be heard(목소리가 ‘들리는’ 수동 — 서4 heard 표적)
10)their = the rule makers of language’s
11)conforming(능동 분사구문 — 서5 ⓑ 정상형)
12)strict(엄격한 — loose 함정)
13)either(either A or B)
14)broke(과거 단일 사건 — 선4 ②)
15)that barrier = the strict expectations of print or broadcast media (the rule makers’ limits)
16)level(평평한=공정한 — 서1 (3) 표적)
17)democratized(수동 과거분사 — 원형 democracy 제공 · 서6 (B)·선4 ③ 표적)
18)Anybody and everybody can become a public figure on the new communicative platforms
19)meaning(분사구문 — 본동사 means 불가)
20)enforce(강제하다 — 서1 (2) 표적)
21)been(might not have been able to — 서2 (1) 표적)
22)can(대조 but — 창작자는 쓸 수 있다)
23)legitimizes(단수 our influence — 원형 제공)
24)gives(both A and B 병렬 수일치 — 서5 ⓓ 표적)
25)a large platform to spread quicker than they could have before(quicker 보충 · 함정어 slower 제외)
26)greater(더 다양한 — narrower 함정, 시험지 선5 표적)
27)variety(다양성)
28)working(현재분사 수식 — 서2 (2) 표적)
29)This = (the fact) that content creators come from a greater variety of backgrounds
30)naturally(동사 go 수식 부사)
31)barriers(장벽 — 선6 재진술 표적)
32)that(선행사 barriers — what 불가)
33)limited(제한했던 — once와 호응)
34)are(주어 networks 복수 — 서5 ⓕ 표적)
35)memes are now transmitted at rapid pace(be→are · transmit→transmitted · now 보충 · 함정어 receive 제외)
