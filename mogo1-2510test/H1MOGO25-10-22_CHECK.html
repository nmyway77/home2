It’s almost impossible to discuss what to do about something changing if you don’t initially know how it works. A patient told of a kidney problem has at least a vague idea of what kidneys are up to, having learned it at school. But that’s not the case for the oceans. When we see a news story about krill in the Southern Ocean becoming scarcer year after year, it sounds generally like a bad thing. But there’s far more to it than the risk of whales going hungry. Krill are a part of the ocean engine. We need to understand at least some of the context before we can discuss the change and take appropriate action.
①Krill help to keep the ocean as a whole system running.
②Krill are the main food source that whales depend on.
③Krill numbers show how quickly the ocean is warming.

22-1 정답 & 4단계 해설 펼치기
정답 ①
1. 📖 문제 해석밑줄 친 ‘크릴은 해양 엔진의 일부이다’가 글에서 뜻하는 바 고르기.
2. 📖 지문/근거 해석바로 앞: 크릴 감소에는 ‘고래가 굶주릴 위험’보다 훨씬 많은 것이 있다. 바로 뒤: 변화를 논의하려면 맥락을 이해해야 한다. 즉 크릴은 고래 먹이 그 이상으로, 해양 전체의 작동에 관여한다.

4. ✦ 정답 도출 + 오답 함정engine = 전체를 움직이는 장치, a part = 그 부품 → ‘해양 체계 전체의 작동에 관여’. ② [한 끗] 사실처럼 보이지만 글은 바로 그 생각(고래 먹이)을 넘어서라고 한다(far more to it than …). ④ [표면 연상] engine의 ‘힘’ 이미지만 따온 선지. ③⑤ 온난화·어획은 언급 없음.

다음 글의 주제로 가장 적절한 것은?
People are at last becoming aware of what we are doing to the ocean, and a public conversation that should have begun decades ago is under way. It will not get far, however, unless a serious gap is filled. Nobody can sensibly debate what to do about a changing system without first knowing how that system works. Thanks to school, a patient hearing bad news about the kidneys at least knows roughly where they are and what they are for. The oceans enjoy no such advantage. News that krill are vanishing from the Southern Ocean sounds bad, but mainly because whales might starve; that krill belong to the ocean engine itself goes unnoticed. Some grasp of the context has to come first, and only then discussion and appropriate action.
①the need to grasp how the ocean works before debating how to protect it
②the reasons public interest in ocean problems has grown so slowly
③the importance of teaching human biology thoroughly in schools

22-서1 정답 & 4단계 해설 펼치기
can we discuss the change and take appropriate action
1. 📖 문제 해석‘맥락을 어느 정도 이해한 뒤에야 변화를 논의하고 적절한 조치를 취할 수 있다’를 Not until 도치 구문으로 전환. 우리말 제시 없음.
2. 📖 지문/근거 해석A before B(B 전에 A가 필요) = Not until A, B(A하고 나서야 비로소 B). 부정어구 Not until ~이 문두에 오면 주절이 「조동사 + 주어 + 동사원형」으로 도치된다.
3. 📖 조건/선지 해석Not until + we understand …(종속절은 정상 어순) + can we discuss the change and take appropriate action = 9단어.
4. ✦ 정답 도출 + 오답 함정도치는 until절이 아니라 주절에서 일어난다(Not until do we understand ✕). we can discuss로 쓰면 도치 누락. and 뒤 take는 discuss와 병렬이므로 원형(taking ✕, can을 반복하지 않음). [채점] 조동사–주어 도치 1.5 · 병렬 take 1 · 나머지 0.5.
