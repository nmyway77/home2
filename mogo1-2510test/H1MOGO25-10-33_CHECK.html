3.0점
다음 글의 밑줄 친 부분 중, 문맥상 낱말의 쓰임이 적절하지 않은 것은?
Despite the cultural trope depicting emotions as the ①opposite of rational thought, cognition — what we commonly refer to as thinking — is actually a key building block of emotion. How we think about our circumstances ②shapes the emotions we experience; then those emotions echo back to influence how we think. For instance, if you walk into a test thinking you are bad at taking tests, your anxiety will be ③reduced. Then you don’t feel good about your performance on the test, and that becomes ④evidence for continuing to think that you’re bad at test taking. In this way there’s simply no pulling emotion and cognition apart. This bi-directionality of cognition and emotion allows us to ⑤adjust difficult emotions by changing the way we think. By thinking differently — I get nervous sometimes, but I’m still a good test taker, or that nervous feeling is just excitement and anticipation, it means I’m ready — you can work those pathways to your advantage.

A cultural trope depicts emotions as the opposite of rational thought, but cognition is actually a key building block of emotion. Despite the cultural trope depicting emotions as the opposite of rational thought, cognition — what we commonly refer to as thinking — is actually a key building block of emotion. How we think about our circumstances shapes the emotions we experience; then those emotions echo back to influence how we think.

(A) In this way there’s simply no pulling emotion and cognition apart. This bi-directionality of cognition and emotion allows us to adjust difficult emotions by changing the way we think.

(B) For instance, if you walk into a test thinking you are bad at taking tests, your anxiety will grow. Then you don’t feel good about your performance on the test, and that becomes evidence for continuing to think that you’re bad at test taking.

33-8 정답 & 4단계 해설 펼치기
정답 ② (B) – (A) – (C)
1. 📖 문제 해석주어진 글 뒤에 이어질 (A)(B)(C)의 순서 정하기.
2. 📖 지문/근거 해석주어진 글: 생각 → 감정 → 다시 생각의 순환. (B) For instance — 시험 예(생각 → 불안 → 나쁜 기분 → 생각의 증거). (A) In this way(이런 식으로) 떼어 놓을 수 없다 → This bi-directionality 덕분에 조절 가능. (C) By thinking differently — (A)의 changing the way we think를 구체화, those pathways.
3. 📖 조건/선지 해석단서: For instance, In this way(앞에 과정 설명 필요), (C)의 thinking differently·those pathways(앞에 양방향성과 ‘생각을 바꿈’이 나와야 함).
4. ✦ 정답 도출 + 오답 함정예시 (B)의 순환을 In this way로 정리하는 (A), 그 끝의 ‘생각하는 방식을 바꿈’을 받아 구체적 방법을 보이는 (C). ③ [한 끗] (B)의 test와 (C)의 test taker가 이어져 보이지만, (C)가 먼저 오면 those pathways가 가리킬 bi-directionality가 아직 나오지 않았고, 마지막에 온 (A)의 In this way가 (C)를 받게 되어 어긋난다.
