The story goes as follows. Once upon a time, a man lived with his old mother. One winter, the man's mother became ill and couldn't eat anything. On one very cold day, the mother said to the man, "It would be wonderful if I (A) [can / could] eat fresh fish." The man went out to the river, but it was completely frozen. It was impossible (B) [for him / of him] to catch any fish. The man was so disappointed that he cried out to the sky, "What should I do? Please help me." Then the ice melted, and three carp suddenly came out of the water. The man went back home and cooked the fish for his mother. Then his mother got well.

"신선한 물고기를 먹을 수 있다면 정말 좋겠구나." / 그가 물고기를 잡는 것은 불가능했습니다.

📌 근거

(A) [학교 프린트 8-④] 주절 It would be wonderful(현재 사실 반대 소망) → if절은 과거형 could. (B) [8-③] 사람의 성격을 나타내는 형용사(kind, nice, wise, careless 등)만 of + 목적격. impossible은 일반 형용사 → for him.

🔍 선지 분석
  • ① ② can — 주절이 would이므로 가정법 과거, can X.
  • ④ of him — impossible은 성격 형용사가 아니다.
  • ⑤ to him — 의미상 주어는 for / of.
⚠️ 함정: "아픈 어머니가 지금 먹고 싶다"는 현재 상황이라 can을 고르기 쉽다. 가정법 과거는 형태는 과거, 의미는 현재.
📘 문법·어휘 포인트

It is + 일반 형용사 + for 목적격 + to V / It is + 성격 형용사 + of 목적격 + to V (It was kind of you to help me.)
