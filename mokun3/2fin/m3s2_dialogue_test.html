[4~6] 다음 대화를 읽고 물음에 답하시오. (L5 Listen & Talk 1)

W: I don't want to walk all the way up there.
M: Come on. Elevators use lots of energy. We need to save energy to protect the environment.
W: But one elevator ride doesn't use that much energy.

So, in my opinion, we need to reduce ⓒthe number of trash cans at school ⓓencouraging recycling. Why don't we place four different colored recycling bins on every floor instead? This will remind students ⓔseparate the paper, glass, plastic, and cans properly.

윗글의 밑줄 친 ⓐ~ⓔ 중 어법상 옳은 것의 개수는? (4점)
① 1개
② 2개
③ 3개
④ 4개
⑤ 5개
정답·해설 보기
정답 ③ (3개: ⓐ, ⓑ, ⓒ)
📖 해석
  • ⓐ ○ instead of + 동명사 recycling
  • ⓑ ○ saves — 주어 it(= recycling) 단수, helps와 병렬
  • ⓒ ○ the number of + 복수명사 (~의 수)
  • ⓓ ✗ encouraging → to encourage — ‘장려하기 위해’ 목적의 to부정사 (원문)
  • ⓔ ✗ separate → to separate — remind + O + to V
📌 근거

ⓓⓔ는 출제를 위한 변형. 프린트 메모: instead of + 동명사 / remind + 목적어 + to부정사.
