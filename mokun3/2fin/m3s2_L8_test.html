다음 글의 밑줄 친 (a)~(e) 중 문맥상 낱말의 쓰임이 적절하지 않은 것은? (3점)
When the fifth man reached the (a)top, he could see the (b)whole forest. Looking at all the paths, he found the (c)longest way out. He (d)hurried down to tell the others. However, when he got to the (e)bottom, everyone was gone.
① (a)
② (b)
③ (c)
④ (d)
⑤ (e)
정답·해설 보기
정답 ③
📖 해석

(c) 숲 전체를 내려다보고 ‘가장 긴’ 출구를 찾았다 → 문맥 모순. 원문 he found the shortest way out(가장 짧은 출구).

📌 근거

높은 곳에서 길을 다 본 목적 = 가장 빠른(짧은) 길 찾기 → 이후 guide가 되어 the quickest ways를 가르침과도 연결. top↔bottom, hurried(서둘러)는 문맥에 맞음.

2
밑줄 친 go on과 의미가 가장 가까운 것은? (3점)
The forest cannot go on forever. A new path will open.
① stop
② disappear
③ grow up
④ continue
⑤ get lost
정답·해설 보기
정답 ④
📖 해석

숲이 영원히 계속될 수는 없어요. 새로운 길이 열릴 거예요.

📌 근거

go on = continue(계속되다). 네 번째 남자가 똑바로 가자는 근거 — 숲은 언젠가 끝난다.

[3~6] 다음 글을 읽고 물음에 답하시오.
One day, five wise men met on the road. They agreed (A)[to travel / traveling] together. On the way, however, they became lost in a thick forest. Before (B)[walk / walking] any further, they decided to stop and find the best way out.
After thinking for a while, the first man said, "I strongly feel that we should go left." The second man said, "We should go right, because right also (C)[mean / means] 'correct.'" Then the third man said, "Let's walk back. We came ⓐthat way, so we can leave that way. Then we can walk around the forest." The fourth man disagreed and said, "I think we should keep walking straight. The forest cannot go on forever. A new path will open."
3
윗글의 (A), (B), (C)에서 어법상 알맞은 것끼리 짝지은 것은? (3점)
① to travel — walk — means
② to travel — walking — means
③ to travel — walking — mean
④ traveling — walking — means
⑤ traveling — walk — mean
정답·해설 보기
정답 ②
📖 해석

(A) agree + to V(~하기로 합의하다) / (B) 전치사 before + 동명사 walking / (C) 주어 right(단어 하나, 3인칭 단수) → means

📌 근거

(C) right은 ‘오른쪽이라는 단어’ — 단수. also가 사이에 있어도 수일치 변화 없음.

② The first man wanted to go right.
③ The third man wanted to climb a tree.

정답·해설 보기
정답 ⑤
📖 해석
  • ① 다섯 남자는 울창한 숲에서 만났다. ✗ (on the road)
  • ② 첫 번째 남자는 오른쪽으로 가고 싶어 했다. ✗ (left)
  • ③ 세 번째 남자는 나무에 오르고 싶어 했다. ✗ (되돌아가기)
  • ④ 두 번째와 네 번째 남자는 서로 동의했다. ✗ (네 번째는 disagreed)
  • ⑤ 네 번째 남자는 새 길이 열릴 거라 생각했다. ○
📌 근거

남자별 방향 교차가 핵심 함정.

6
윗글의 밑줄 친 keep walking straight와 의미가 같은 것은? (4점)
① stop walking straight
② continue to walk straight
③ keep to walk straight
④ remember walking straight
⑤ try walking back
정답·해설 보기
정답 ②
📖 해석

우리가 계속 똑바로 걸어가야 한다고 생각해요.

📌 근거

keep + -ing = continue + to V / -ing(계속 ~하다). keep은 to부정사를 목적어로 쓰지 않는다(③ ✗). ④ remember -ing는 ‘~한 것을 기억하다’.

ⓐLooking at them all, the fifth man shook his head and said, "I know how to solve this. Just wait." He started to climb ⓑthe tallest tree he could find. ⓒAs he climbed, everyone else decided to go their separate ways. When the fifth man ⓓreached to the top, he could see the whole forest. Looking at all the paths, he found the shortest way out. He hurried down ⓔtell the others. ( ① ) He thought to himself, Where did they go? I found the best way out. ( ② ) He thought they were all wrong and he was the only wise man. ( ③ ) However, he was wrong. ( ④ ) Everyone was wise. ( ⑤ ) Each man had chosen his path and created his future.

윗글의 밑줄 친 ⓐ~ⓔ 중 어법상 옳은 것의 개수는? (4점)
① 1개
② 2개
③ 3개
④ 4개
⑤ 5개
정답·해설 보기
정답 ③ (3개: ⓐ, ⓑ, ⓒ)
📖 해석
  • ⓐ ○ Looking — 분사구문(다섯 번째 남자가 바라보는 능동)
  • ⓑ ○ the tallest tree (that) he could find — 최상급 + 관계대명사 생략
  • ⓒ ○ As — ~하는 동안(접속사)
  • ⓓ ✗ reached to → reached — reach는 타동사(= got to / arrived at)

[11~14] 다음 글을 읽고 물음에 답하시오.
The man who went to the left walked (A)[deep / deeper] into the forest. He was attacked by wild animals and almost died of hunger. Soon he learned how to protect (B)[him / himself] and find food. In the end, he made his way out of the forest and taught others survival skills.
The man who went to the right met a group of thieves. They took everything from him and made him (C)[join / to join] them. While he was with them, he showed them great kindness. The thieves were moved by his kindness and learned from his wisdom. Later, they also became wise men.
The man who walked back created a safe path around the forest. Using this path, people could get where they were going without getting lost, even though the trip took a little longer.

② deep — himself — to join

정답·해설 보기
정답 ④
📖 해석

(A) 숲속으로 ‘더 깊이’ → 비교급 deeper(원문) / (B) protect의 목적어가 주어 he 자신 → 재귀대명사 himself / (C) 사역동사 make + O + 원형 → made him join

② 이동이 조금 더 오래 걸렸지만 길을 잃지 않고 도착할 수 있었다.
③ 이동이 짧아서 사람들이 길을 잃었다.
④ 이동이 오래 걸려서 사람들이 그 길을 이용하지 않았다.
⑤ 이동 시간은 이전과 같았다.
정답·해설 보기
정답 ②
📖 해석

이 길을 이용해서, 사람들은 비록 이동이 조금 더 오래 걸렸지만 길을 잃지 않고 가려던 곳에 도착할 수 있었다.

even though = 비록 ~이지만(양보). 안전함(장점) ↔ 조금 더 오래 걸림(단점)의 대조. ①은 양보를 이유(because)로 바꾼 오답.
