② Q: Why have architects in Singapore begun to design eco-friendly buildings?
A: Because most buildings in Singapore need air conditioning, which uses a lot of energy and contributes to climate change.
③ Q: How does an open structure allow a building to stay cool?
A: The greenery of the large gardens protects the building from direct sunlight.
④ Q: What are the goals of this new style of architecture?
A: The goals are to help protect the environment and provide people with a good quality of life.
⑤ Q: What do they have in common?
A: They are made to help protect the environment.
