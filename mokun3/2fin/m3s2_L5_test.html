[1~2] 어휘 — 학교 프린트 Lesson 5 Vocabulary 영영풀이 원문

1
단어와 영영풀이가 바르게 연결된 것은? (3점)
① prevent: the top layer of something
② additional: more than what is already present or available
③ structure: a new or better idea, method, or device
④ innovation: the basic form or arrangement of something
⑤ surface: to stop something from happening
정답·해설 보기
정답 ②
📖 해석
  • ① prevent: 어떤 것의 맨 위층 ✗ (surface의 풀이)
  • ② additional: 이미 있거나 이용 가능한 것보다 더 많은 ○
  • ③ structure: 새롭거나 더 나은 아이디어·방법·장치 ✗ (innovation)
  • ④ innovation: 어떤 것의 기본 형태나 배열 ✗ (structure)
  • ⑤ surface: 어떤 일이 일어나지 못하게 막다 ✗ (prevent)
📌 근거

프린트 영영풀이 원문 기준. ①↔⑤, ③↔④ 풀이를 서로 맞바꾼 교차형.

⚠️ 함정: structure ↔ innovation 교차는 둘 다 ‘형태/방법’ 같은 추상어라 헷갈리기 쉬움.
2
밑줄 친 contributes to의 영영풀이로 알맞은 것은? (3점)
Most buildings need air conditioning, which uses a lot of energy and contributes to climate change.
① to stop working properly
② to divide into simpler substances
③ capable of being used more than once
④ to play a role in the cause or result of something
⑤ information to help people understand something
정답·해설 보기
정답 ④
📖 해석

대부분의 건물은 에어컨이 필요한데, 그것은 많은 에너지를 쓰고 기후 변화의 원인이 된다. → contribute to = 어떤 것의 원인이나 결과에 역할을 하다

📌 근거

contribute to는 ‘기여하다’와 ‘~의 원인이 되다’ 둘 다 — 본문에서는 기후 변화의 원인(부정적 결과)으로 쓰임.

🔍 선택지·오답 분석
  • ① break down(고장 나다)의 2번 풀이
  • ② break down(분해되다)의 1번 풀이
  • ③ reusable
  • ⑤ explanation
[3~6] 다음 글을 읽고 물음에 답하시오.
It is important for us to find ways to protect the environment. Some people ⓐhave found creative ways to save the earth. One example is an underwater museum in Cancun, Mexico. ... Cancun is a city ⓑwhich 4.8 million tourists travel every year. One of the most popular activities to do there ⓒare looking at the area's beautiful sea life underwater. However, tourist activities are seriously damaging parts of the sea near Cancun.
To prevent (A)this, artists did something interesting. They thought if they attracted tourists to a different part of the sea, the dying areas could have time to get better. They made an underwater museum away from the places where sea life was dying. It's about 14 meters below the surface and contains 500 statues. The statues ⓓare made from materials that support sea life. They provide additional places for plants and animals ⓔto live on.

윗글의 밑줄 친 ⓐ~ⓔ 중 어법상 옳은 것의 개수는? (4점)
① 1개
② 2개
③ 3개
④ 4개
⑤ 5개
정답·해설 보기
정답 ③ (3개: ⓐ, ⓓ, ⓔ)
📖 해석
  • ⓐ ○ have found — 현재완료(결과), 복수 주어 Some people
  • ⓑ ✗ which → where — 뒤 절 4.8 million tourists travel이 완전한 절(자동사) → 관계부사 (= in which)
  • ⓒ ✗ are → is — 주어는 One(of the most popular activities)
  • ⓓ ○ be made from — 재료가 변형된 원료(~로 만들어지다)
  • ⓔ ○ to live on — places를 꾸미는 형용사적 to부정사, live on places의 전치사 on 유지
📌 근거

관계부사 vs 관계대명사(뒤 절이 완전한가) / One of + 복수명사 → 단수 동사.

⚠️ 함정: ⓔ to live on의 on을 빼면 틀림(live on the places). 형용사적 to부정사의 전치사 생략 금지.

② Tourists looking at sea life underwater
③ Tourist activities damaging parts of the sea near Cancun
④ Artists making 500 statues
⑤ Sea life growing on the statues
정답·해설 보기
정답 ③
📖 해석

To prevent this — 이것을 막기 위해. 막아야 할 대상 = 바로 앞 문장 관광 활동이 칸쿤 근처 바다를 심각하게 훼손하는 것.

📌 근거

prevent(막다)의 목적어가 될 수 있는 부정적 상황은 ③뿐. ②는 원인이 되는 활동이지 막으려는 ‘훼손’ 자체가 아님.

[7~10] 다음 글을 읽고 물음에 답하시오.
Singapore is hot throughout the year. Most buildings need air conditioning, (A)[which / that] uses a lot of energy and contributes to climate change. That's why architects in Singapore (B)[has / have] begun to design eco-friendly buildings that use less air conditioning but are still cool inside. For example, many buildings in Singapore are designed to have an open structure. This structure makes it possible (C)[for / of] outside air to move throughout a building.
7
윗글의 (A), (B), (C)에서 어법상 알맞은 것끼리 짝지은 것은? (3점)
① which — has — for
② which — have — for
③ which — have — of
④ that — has — of
⑤ that — have — for
정답·해설 보기
정답 ②
📖 해석

(A) 콤마 뒤 계속적 용법 → which (that 불가) / (B) 주어 architects(복수) → have begun / (C) possible은 사람의 성격 형용사가 아님 → 의미상 주어 for outside air

📌 근거

계속적 용법(, which)에는 that을 쓸 수 없다. in Singapore는 수식어구라 수일치에 영향 없음.

⚠️ 함정: (B) 바로 앞 Singapore(단수)에 끌려 has를 고르게 하는 함정.
8
윗글의 밑줄 친 That's why 문장과 의미가 같은 것은? (4점)
That's why architects in Singapore have begun to design eco-friendly buildings ...
① That's the reason why architects have begun to design ...
② That's the reason which architects have begun to design ...
③ That's the way how architects have begun to design ...
④ That's because architects have begun to design ...
⑤ That's for which architects have begun to design ...
정답·해설 보기
정답 ①
📖 해석

그것이 싱가포르의 건축가들이 친환경 건물을 설계하기 시작한 이유이다. (That = 에어컨이 에너지를 많이 쓰고 기후 변화의 원인이 되는 것)

📌 근거

That's why + 결과 / That's because + 원인. 앞 문장이 원인, 뒤가 결과이므로 why.

🔍 선택지·오답 분석
  • ② the reason which ✗ — 뒤 절이 완전 → 관계부사 why / for which
  • ③ the way how ✗ — 함께 쓸 수 없음, 또 ‘방법’으로 의미가 다름
  • ④ That's because ✗ — 원인·결과가 뒤바뀜(그것은 ~때문이다)
  • ⑤ for which 단독 ✗ — 선행사 the reason이 필요

윗글의 밑줄 친 문장과 의미가 같도록 바꿔 쓴 것으로 알맞은 것은? (4점)
Eco-friendly buildings like these not only help protect the environment, but also provide people with a good quality of life.
① ... provide people with a good quality of life as well as help protect the environment.
② ... help protect the environment as well as provide people with a good quality of life.
③ ... provide people to a good quality of life as well as help protect the environment.
④ ... provide a good quality of life with people as well as help protect the environment.

정답·해설 보기
정답 ①
📖 해석

  • ③ provide people to ✗

  • ⑤ both A or B ✗ (both A and B)

③ (B) — (C) — (A)
④ (C) — (A) — (B)
⑤ (C) — (B) — (A)

정답 ④
📖 해석
