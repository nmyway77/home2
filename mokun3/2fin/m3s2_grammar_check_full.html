[2025 #28형] (A)~(E) 중 어법상 옳은 것을 두 개 고르면?
(A) It is wise of you to have prepared in advance.
(B) The milk was too hot for the baby to drink it.
(C) Walked down the road, she met her brother.
(D) The higher the price gets, the fewer people will buy the product.
(E) If we had the time, we will go to Europe.
① (A)
② (B)
③ (C)
④ (D)
⑤ (E)
정답·해설·해석
정답 ①, ④
📖 해석
  • (A) ○ 성품 형용사 wise → of + 완료 to부정사
  • (B) ✗ 문장 주어(The milk)와 같은 목적어 it 삭제 → for the baby to drink
  • (C) ✗ 그녀가 ‘걷는’ 능동 → Walking
  • (D) ○ the 비교급 ~, the 비교급 …
  • (E) ✗ 가정법 과거 → we would go

[2024 #23형] 분사구문을 바르게 만든 것은?
① When I saw a dog, I was so happy. → Seen a dog, I was so happy.
② Because he had lost his wallet, he couldn’t pay for lunch. → Having lost his wallet, he couldn’t pay for lunch.
③ Because she hadn’t finished her homework, she couldn’t go out. → Having not finished her homework, she couldn’t go out.
④ If it is used properly, this tool is very useful. → Using properly, this tool is very useful.
⑤ Because he was surprised by the news, he couldn’t say anything. → Surprising by the news, he couldn’t say anything.
정답·해설·해석
정답 ②
📖 해석
  • ① 내가 ‘본’ 능동 → Seeing
  • ② ○ 주절보다 앞선 일 → Having p.p.
  • ③ not은 분사 앞 → Not having finished
  • ④ 도구가 ‘사용되는’ 수동 → (Being) Used
  • ⑤ 그가 ‘놀란’ → (Being) Surprised
