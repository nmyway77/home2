[이해형 서답 CHECK] 전치사 되살리기 · 분사구문 되돌리기

16
관계부사를 ‘전치사 + which’로 바꿀 때 알맞은 전치사를 쓰시오.
(1) This is the house where I grew up. → the house in which
(2) I remember the summer when we met. → the summer during which
(3) That is the stage where the band performed. → the stage on which
(4) Tell me the reason why you were late. → the reason for which
정답·해설 보기
정답 (1) in (2) during (3) on (4) for
📌 근거

뒤 절을 원래 문장으로 되돌려 전치사 확인: grow up in the house / during the summer / perform on the stage / for the reason. 2024 #26형의 바탕.

17
다음 분사구문을 접속사가 있는 절로 바꿔 쓰시오. (온전한 문장)
(1) Having finished the report, she went home. → After she had finished the report, she went home.
(2) Used properly, this app saves a lot of time. → If it is used properly, this app saves a lot of time.
정답·해설 보기
정답 (1) After(When/Because) she had finished the report (2) If it is used properly
📌 근거

Having p.p. = 주절보다 앞선 일 → had p.p. / Used = 수동 → it is used. 2024 #23형(분사구문 정오)의 판단 근거.

18
다음 문장을 ‘the 비교급 ~, the 비교급 …’ 구문으로 바꿔 쓰시오.
If more people share rides, there will be fewer cars on the road.
→ The more people share rides, the fewer cars there will be on the road.
정답·해설 보기
정답 The more people share rides, the fewer cars there will be on the road.
📌 근거

비교급 + 명사(more people / fewer cars)는 명사를 데리고 절 앞으로 이동. cars 가산 → fewer(less ✗).
